# Importing Your Existing Wardrobe

Already use another digital closet app or have online shopping receipts? You can bring your existing wardrobe into DressApp in minutes.

## Ways to Import Your Clothes

### 1. Wardrobe Migration Agent & Migration Hub (Manager & Professional Plans)
- Open the **Wardrobe Migration Hub** (`/migration`) or select **Import Wardrobe** in your **Profile** settings.
- **Choose & Connect**: Pick your competitor wardrobe platform (such as Whering, Acloset, Stylebook, Smartli, Indyx, BeautyAI) or enter any wardrobe web URL.
- **In-App Monitored Browser**: DressApp opens the platform in an embedded, monitored browser session.
- **Close Pop-ups & Navigate**: Sign in to your account, dismiss any cookie notices or promotional pop-up windows, and navigate to your closet or wardrobe grid.
- **Movable DressApp Agent Floater**: A compact, draggable DressApp Agent icon floats on the screen. Drag it anywhere out of the way so it never blocks login forms or pop-up close buttons.
- **Tap to Migrate**: When you are on your wardrobe grid, tap the DressApp Agent icon and click **Start Wardrobe Migration**.
- **Automated AI Ingestion**: The agent scans the grid, crops each garment card, removes backgrounds via GarmentVision, applies AI tags, and saves them directly to your DressApp closet database.
- **Batch Screenshots / Video Fallback**: You can also upload closet screenshots or screen recordings directly from your device's photo gallery.

### 2. Import from Online Store Links or Digital Receipts
- Paste a product link from an online clothing store, or upload an order confirmation screenshot or PDF receipt.
- DressApp automatically extracts each item's photo, brand, size, and price.

### 3. Automatic Cleanup & Duplicate Protection
- DressApp's AI removes the background from every imported item, fills in its category, colors, and season, and automatically skips duplicate items so your closet stays neat.
