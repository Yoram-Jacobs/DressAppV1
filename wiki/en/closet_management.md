# Virtual Closet Command Center

Manage, search, and organize your digitized wardrobe with ease.

## Overview
The Closet workspace displays your entire wardrobe in an instant-paint grid. You can browse all your clothes, search by color or style, organize items with bulk Group Tagging, and combine pieces into coordinated sets.

## Prerequisites
- At least one digitized garment in your closet.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Browse Your Wardrobe**: Scroll through your closet grid. Use the category chips (*Tops*, *Bottoms*, *Outerwear*, *Shoes*, *Accessories*, *Dresses*) to filter items in seconds.
2. **Search Easily**: Enter keywords (e.g., *"blue sweater"*, *"linen"*) or type natural descriptive phrases (*"something cozy for autumn"*) to search by visual-semantic style.
3. **Group Tagging (Bulk Organization)**:
   - Tap the **Select / Batch Edit** button in the header toolbar.
   - Tap individual clothes or tap *"Select all Tops"*.
   - Tap **Group Tag** in the bottom floating bar.
   - Pick the seasons (*Spring*, *Summer*, *Fall*, *Winter*), formality (*Casual*, *Smart Casual*, *Business*, *Formal*), or type custom tags (*"Vacation"*, *"Office"*).
   - Tap **Apply to Selected Items** to update all items at once!
4. **Create Coordinated Sets**: Drag and drop matching garments onto each other to link them as a synchronized set (like a two-piece suit).
5. **Edit Item Details**: Tap any garment card to open the Item Details editor to update tags, prices, or take clean cutouts.

## Expected Results
A beautifully organized closet grid that loads instantly, letting you find, filter, and tag dozens of garments in seconds.

## Troubleshooting
- **Grey or blank cards**: If an image preview looks grey, tap **Repair Thumbnails** in your settings to regenerate card pictures.
- **Accidental grouping**: If you grouped two items by mistake, open the item details and tap **Ungroup** to separate them back into individual pieces.

## Limitations
- Free tier accounts can store up to 150 garments. To store more, invite friends (+10 slots per signup) or upgrade to Pro for unlimited storage.
