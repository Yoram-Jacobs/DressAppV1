# Outfit Planner & 2D Canvas

Compose, layer, and preview coordinated outfits on your virtual avatar before getting dressed.

## Overview
The Outfit Planner provides an interactive 2D avatar canvas where you can test-fit clothes together. You can preview tops, bottoms, shoes, and accessories layered on your avatar, toggle outerwear on and off, and check compatibility scores before stepping out the door.

## Prerequisites
- Digitized clothes in your closet.
- (Optional) A full-body photo in your profile if you prefer trying clothes on your real silhouette.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Open the Canvas**: Tap the **Outfit Canvas** tab in the app.
2. **Dress Your Avatar**: Drag and drop or tap garments to position them on the mannequin:
   - Tops fit cleanly against the neckline.
   - Pants and skirts align comfortably with the natural waist.
   - Shoes sit neatly on the foot plane.
3. **Outerwear Layering ("With / Without Outerwear")**:
   - Add a jacket, cardigan, or overcoat.
   - Use the toggle to view your outfit **With Outerwear** (jacket closed over shirt) or **Without Outerwear** (shirt and pants visible underneath).
4. **Tap for Details**: Tap any garment directly on your avatar's body to open its detail card or swap it for an alternative.
5. **Check Compatibility Scores**: Review the harmony bars showing how well the colors, fabrics, weather suitability, and formality match.
6. **Save to Calendar**: Tap **Save Look** to schedule the outfit for today or any upcoming date.

## Expected Results
A realistic, beautifully layered preview of your outfit that eliminates guesswork and prevents morning wardrobe changes.

## Troubleshooting
- **Coat appears behind shirt**: Ensure your jacket is categorized as "Outerwear" so the system automatically layers it on top of inner shirts.
- **Avatar looks misaligned**: Open your Profile to check your height and weight settings, or tap "Reset Avatar" to restore default mannequin proportions.

## Limitations
- Outerwear automatically layers over tops; specialized custom layering orders (e.g. wearing a shirt over a hoodie) are not yet manually draggable.
