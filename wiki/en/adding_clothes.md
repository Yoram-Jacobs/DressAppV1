# Ingesting & Adding Clothes

Digitize your physical closet in seconds using our multiple import paradigms.

## Overview
Ingest clothes using live camera snaps, file uploads, Digital Product Passports (DPP) QR tags, or digital receipts (OCR extraction).

## Prerequisites
- Clear, well-lit photos of individual garments.
- PDF or image digital receipts.
- Camera access for scanning QR codes.

## Step-by-Step
1. **Interactive Upload**: Snap a photo or drag files into the **Camera & Upload** tab.
2. **Live Matting**: The AI segments the garment, removes backgrounds, and auto-fills 20+ fields (size, colors, fabrics).
3. **Category Adjustments**: If the auto-category is incorrect, update it in the dropdown; the cutout automatically re-polishes for the new category.
4. **Digital Receipt Import**: Switch to the **Digital Import** tab to parse online order invoices, locking transaction fields automatically.
5. **DPP Scan**: Tap **Scan QR** to instantly read manufacturer composition and care facts.

## Expected Results
Garments appear instantly in your Closet with clean, transparent cutouts and fully indexed search attributes.

## Troubleshooting
- **Double-cut outline**: Ensure the garment is flat and photographed against a contrasting solid wall or floor.
- **Receipt OCR fails**: Manually highlight the receipt text fields using the adjustable overlay selectors.

## Limitations
- Single uploads are capped at 45 seconds; large batch uploads (>5 items) route to sequential background queues to avoid timeouts.
