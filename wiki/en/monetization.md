# Subscription Plans & Credits

DressApp offers a generous Free plan alongside flexible monthly and annual subscriptions and optional credit packs.

## Plan Overview

1. **Free Plan**:
   - Store up to **50 closet items** for free—and expand your closet up to **150 items** by sharing your referral link with friends (**+10 free slots** for every friend who signs up).
   - Powered right out of the box by **DressApp's on-premises AI model** (**10 daily credits**, accumulating up to **300 credits over 30 days**) plus **5 starter Studio Reconstruction credits**.
   - Supports single-garment photos and full-outfit photos worn by a person. **Cluster garment uploading** (closets, hangers, shoe racks, or piles of shirts) is disabled on the Free plan because a single cluster image can consume your entire Free reconstruction allowance at once, leaving many garments unreconstructed.

2. **Manager Plan ($10/month or $100/year)**:
   - Unlocks **unlimited closet space**, priority access to DressApp's **cutting-edge AI model**, **100 monthly Studio Reconstruction credits**, **Cluster Garment Uploading** (photograph garments hanging in your closet, shoe racks, or shirt piles), daily morning outfit alerts, Calendar sync, Marketplace selling, and full access to **Trend Scout**.

3. **Professional Plan ($15/month or $150/year)**:
   - Designed for professional stylists, tailors, and fashion creators.
   - Includes everything in the Manager plan, plus a verified listing in the **Stylist Directory**, the ability to launch promotional campaigns, priority support, and **100 monthly styling and reconstruction credits**.

## Plan Demotion & Closet Retention Warning
- **Manager and Professional users who demote to the Free plan will lose their extra closet slots, leaving only the last 50 added items in their closet.**
- Any older closet assets beyond your **50 most recently added items** will be permanently deleted when your plan switches to Free.
- Before accepting a planned demotion or subscription cancellation, DressApp displays a clear warning showing how many items will be affected and asks for your explicit confirmation.

## How to Upgrade or Manage Your Plan
1. Open your **Profile** page and scroll to **Subscription & Limits**.
2. Click the **View Plans** or **Pricing** link.
3. Choose your preferred plan to upgrade securely, or review the confirmation warning if switching back to the Free plan.
