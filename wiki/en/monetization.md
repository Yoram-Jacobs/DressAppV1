# Subscriptions, Credit Packs & Billing

Understand your account limits, upgrade your closet space, explore the Tester Group Program, and manage your AI styling credits.

## Overview
DressApp offers a flexible hybrid monetization model designed to welcome casual closet organizers while providing powerful tools for fashion enthusiasts, creators, and certified stylists:
- **Free Tier Baseline**: Store up to 50 garments for free (expandable up to 150 items via referral bonuses) with 5 complimentary onboarding AI reconstruction credits. Powered out-of-the-box by Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite with zero API key setup.
- **Manager Plan ($10/month or $100/year)**: Unlimited wardrobe capacity, morning outfit push scheduler, Trend Scout fashion radar, circular marketplace selling, and 100 monthly AI credits.
- **Professional Plan ($15/month or $150/year)**: Tailored for solo stylists and influencers, adding sponsored Ad Campaign creation, expert directory listing, priority support, and 100 monthly AI credits.
- **Tester Group Program**: Approved tester emails automatically enjoy complimentary access to the full Professional tier with zero subscription fees.
- **Prepaid Credit Packs**: Top up with non-expiring credit packs ($3 for 10, $12 for 50, $20 for 100) whenever high-volume styling or photo reconstruction is needed.

## Prerequisites
- A registered DressApp account.
- A PayPal account, credit/debit card, or the Israeli Bit app (for local ILS checkout) for plan upgrades or credit top-ups.
- *(For Testers)* Registration using an approved tester email address.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Explore the Free Baseline**: Start organizing immediately with 50 items and 5 onboarding credits. Zero API keys are needed.
2. **Expand for Free via Referrals**: Share your personal invite link from Profile settings. Each friend who joins unlocks +10 permanent closet slots (up to 150 items max).
3. **Tester Group Verification**: If you are part of the DressApp beta tester group (`maystarboard@gmail.com`, `lokoprod@gmail.com`, `dressapdeveloper@gmail.com`), sign in via Google OAuth to automatically receive complimentary **Professional Plan** privileges.
4. **Upgrade to Manager ($10/mo or $100/yr)**: Tap **Upgrade Plan** on the Pricing page to unlock unlimited garments, morning scheduler alerts, and 100 monthly AI credits.
5. **Upgrade to Professional ($15/mo or $150/yr)**: Select Professional to create targeted fashion advertising campaigns, reach local clients, and gain priority stylist support.
6. **Purchase Prepaid Credits (Never Expire)**: If monthly credits run out, purchase a 10, 50, or 100 credit pack. The system always consumes monthly subscription credits first, protecting your permanent non-expiring balance!

## Expected Results
Instant activation of plan benefits upon subscription or tester recognition. Closet limits immediately update to unlimited, and badges reflect your active tier on your Profile dashboard.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Closet Limit Reached" alert**: You have filled your available slots (50 baseline or up to 150 with referrals). Archive unworn items, invite friends for extra slots, or upgrade to Manager/Pro for unlimited capacity.
- **Tester perks not showing**: Ensure you signed in with your exact allow-listed tester email address. The backend grants the Professional tier upon token issuance.
- **Credit pack not reflected after payment**: Refresh your Profile page; transaction webhooks capture and credit your account within seconds.

## Limitations
- Monthly subscription credits do not roll over to subsequent billing cycles; purchased prepaid credit packs, however, never expire.
- Referral capacity bonuses apply up to a maximum cap of 150 items on the Free Tier.
