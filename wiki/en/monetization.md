# Subscription Plans & Credits

DressApp offers a generous Free plan alongside flexible monthly subscriptions and optional credit packs.

## Plan Overview

1. **Free Plan**:
   - Store up to **50 closet items** for free—and expand your closet up to **150 items** by sharing your referral link with friends (**+10 free slots** for every friend who signs up).
   - Powered right out of the box by **DressApp's on-premises AI model** with no technical setup required.

2. **Manager Plan ($10/month or $100/year)**:
   - Unlocks **unlimited closet space**, priority access to DressApp's **cutting-edge AI model**, daily morning outfit alerts, Google Calendar sync, Marketplace selling, and full access to **Trend Scout**.

3. **Professional Plan ($15/month or $150/year)**:
   - Designed for professional stylists, tailors, and fashion creators.
   - Includes everything in the Manager plan, plus a verified listing in the **Stylist Directory**, the ability to launch local promotional campaigns, priority support, and **100 monthly styling credits**.

## How to Upgrade
1. Open your **Profile** page and scroll to **Subscription & Closet Space**.
2. Click the **Pricing** or **Upgrade** button.
3. Choose your preferred plan and complete checkout securely via PayPal.
