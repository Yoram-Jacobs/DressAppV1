# Subscriptions, Credit Packs & Billing

Understand your account limits, upgrade your closet space, and manage your AI styling credits.

## Overview
DressApp offers a generous free tier alongside premium options for wardrobe lovers and professional stylists. You can store up to 50 items for free baseline (expandable up to 150 items via referral bonuses), upgrade to unlimited closet storage with Pro, or purchase prepaid credit packs that never expire.

## Prerequisites
- A verified DressApp account.
- A PayPal account, credit card, or Israeli Bit app for subscription upgrades or credit pack purchases.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Understand Your Free Tier**:
   - Every free account starts with **50 garments** baseline.
   - You receive **5 free onboarding AI reconstruction credits** upon signup (30-day lifespan).
   - Zero friction: **no personal Google Gemini key is ever required**.
   - If your free reconstruction credits hit 0, you'll see:
     *"You've used your free AI reconstructions! Upgrade to Manager for automated access or purchase a credit pack."*
2. **Upgrade to Manager ($10/month or $100/year)**:
   - Tap **Upgrade Plan** on the Pricing page.
   - Enjoy **unlimited closet space**, unlimited daily requests, marketplace selling & renting, Trend Scout access, scheduler & push notifications, and **100 AI credits / month (not cumulative)**.
   - When credits reach 0, you'll see: *"You've used your AI reconstructions! Purchase a credit pack to continue."*
3. **Upgrade to Professional ($15/month or $150/year)**:
   - Built for solo stylists, creators, and expert consultants.
   - Includes all Manager features + **Ad Campaigns creation**, dedicated priority support, and **100 AI credits / month (not cumulative)**.
   - When credits reach 0, you'll see: *"You've used your AI reconstructions! Purchase a credit pack to continue."*
4. **Prepaid AI Credit Packs (Never Expire)**:
   - If you need extra styling power or on-demand reconstructions, purchase a prepaid pack:
     - **10 Credits**: $3.00
     - **50 Credits**: $12.00
     - **100 Credits**: $20.00 (*Best Value*)
   - **Smart Spending**: The system always consumes your monthly subscription/trial credits first, protecting your permanent paid credits which never expire!
5. **Local Israeli Checkout (Atzmai Gateway)**:
   - Users in Israel can pay conveniently in ILS using **Bit** or local credit cards, receiving official tax invoices and receipts via email.

## Expected Results
Instant activation of additional closet slots or AI credits, with real-time updates to your profile badges and receipt confirmation sent to your email.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Closet Limit Reached" Warning**: You have reached the capacity limit (50 items baseline, up to 150 with referral slots). Either delete unused clothes, share your invite link for free slots, or upgrade to Pro for unlimited storage.
- **Credits balance not showing immediately**: Refresh your Profile page; paid credits update within a few seconds after payment confirmation.

## Limitations
- Daily free credits expire after 30 days if unused, whereas purchased paid credits never expire.
- Referral bonuses apply to verified new user registrations up to a maximum ceiling of 150 garments.
