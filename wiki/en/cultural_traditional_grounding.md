# Cultural & Traditional Attire Grounding Guide

## Overview
DressApp provides culturally grounded styling intelligence across 13 languages. Rather than relying on small-language-model probabilistic guesses—which frequently hallucinate or confuse distinct religious rites—DressApp enforces explicit, human-audited **Ground-Truth Axioms**. 

This document defines the canonical attire protocols, silhouette requirements, strict negative prohibitions, and localized linguistic terminologies across global traditions.

---

## 1. Jewish Traditions (יהדות)

### 1.1 Shiva & Mourning Etiquette (שבעה, אבלות וניחום אבלים)
- **Occasion Type**: Somber, condolence call, memorial, funeral.
- **Formality**: Respectful, solemn, understated.
- **Color Palette**: Solid black, charcoal, dark slate, deep navy, dark brown, or subdued muted earth tones. Avoid bright primaries, pastels, white, gold, or red.
- **Silhouettes & Cuts**:
  - **Men**: Clean tailored trousers or dark unadorned jeans; plain dark collared button-down, polo, or neat crewneck tee; dark subdued shoes or loafers.
  - **Women**: Modest midi/maxi dresses, long skirts, or tailored trousers; tops with sleeves covering shoulders and upper arms; conservative neckline.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO graphic t-shirts, loud logos, eagle/animal prints, slogan tees, or comedy elements.
  - NO shorts, distressed/ripped denim, gym/workout wear, or beach slides.
  - NO vibrant red, yellow, hot pink, gold, or neon colors.
  - NO flashy luxury jewelry or statement watches.
- **Language & Cultural Lexicon**:
  - *Approved Hebrew Terms*: `לביקור שבעה`, `לניחום אבלים`, `לשבעה`, `בשבעה`.
  - *Prohibited Machine-Translation Artifacts*: Never use literal translations like `להולך בישיבה שבעה` or `בישיבה שבעה`.

### 1.2 Orthodox Modesty (צניעות - Tzniut)
- **Occasion Type**: Daily, synagogue, community, sacred gatherings.
- **Formality**: Conservative, dignified.
- **Requirements**:
  - **Women**: Skirts and dresses extending below the knee even when seated; sleeves extending past the elbow; neckline covering the collarbone; non-transparent and non-clinging fabrics. For married Orthodox women, head covering (sheitel, tichel, or hat).
  - **Men**: Long trousers, collared shirts with sleeves, head covering (kippah / yarmulke).
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO trousers, jeans, or shorts for orthodox women.
  - NO sleeveless or cap-sleeve tops without an opaque layer (blazer/cardigan).
  - NO deep V-necks, low backs, sheer textiles, or high slit skirts.

### 1.3 Shabbat & Festive Holidays (שבת וחגים)
- **Occasion Type**: Joyous religious celebration, Friday evening dinner, holiday services.
- **Formality**: Elevated smart-casual to formal.
- **Color Palette**: Crisp white, cream, navy, sky blue, jewel tones, refined pastels.
- **Silhouettes**:
  - **Men**: Crisp white or light blue collared shirt, tailored dress trousers, blazer or sports jacket, polished dress shoes.
  - **Women**: Festive elegant midi/maxi dresses, tailored skirts with fine blouses, elegant accessories.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO workwear, athletic apparel, distressed clothing, or casual loungewear.

## 2. Islamic Traditions (الإسلام)

### 2.1 Jumu'ah & Mosque Visitation (صلاة الجمعة وزيارة المسجد)
- **Occasion Type**: Friday congregational prayer, sacred mosque entry.
- **Formality**: Clean, modest, respectful.
- **Requirements**:
  - **Men**: Clean long trousers, thobe, or kurta; shoulders and chest fully covered; clean socks (shoes removed at entrance).
  - **Women**: Loose-fitting abaya, maxi dress, or long tunic over loose trousers; full coverage to wrists and ankles; hijab covering hair, ears, and neck.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO shorts above or at the knee for men (awrah requirement).
  - NO tight bodycon clothing, sheer fabrics, or low-cut necklines.
  - NO clothing depicting faces, human idols, or animal prints inside prayer spaces.
- **Language & Lexicon**:
  - *Arabic Terms*: `لصلاة الجمعة`, `لزيارة المسجد`, `لباس محتشم ولائق`.

### 2.2 Everyday Modest Dressing (الحشمة والحجاب)
- **Formality**: Casual to smart-casual.
- **Requirements**: Opaque fabrics, relaxed silhouettes (A-line, palazzo, long trench, kimonos/abayas), high necklines, long sleeves.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO sheer or mesh cutouts without opaque inner linings.

## 3. Hindu Traditions (सनातन धर्म)

### 3.1 Funerals & Mourning (Antyeshti - अंतिम संस्कार)
- **Occasion Type**: Hindu cremation, funeral procession, bereavement.
- **Formality**: Solemn, pure, austere.
- **Color Palette**: **Solid, plain, unadorned white only**.
- **Silhouettes**:
  - **Men**: Plain white kurta-pyjama or white cotton shirt with light neutral pants.
  - **Women**: Simple white cotton saree or white salwar kameez without embellishment.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION ON BLACK**: In Hindu tradition, black is considered inauspicious and strictly forbidden at funerals.
  - NO vibrant colors (red, gold, orange, pink).
  - NO leather shoes or leather belts inside sacred cremation grounds or temple rituals.

### 3.2 Weddings & Festive Celebrations (Vivaha & Diwali - विवाह उत्सव)
- **Occasion Type**: Hindu wedding ceremonies (Sangeet, Baraat, Pheras), Diwali, auspicious festivals.
- **Formality**: Opulent, celebratory, high formal.
- **Color Palette**: Auspicious reds, deep maroons, gold, saffron, royal emerald, rani pink.
- **Silhouettes**: Sherwani, Bandhgala, Kurta with Nehru jacket for men; Lehenga, Kanjeevaram/Banarasi saree, Anarkali for women.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **PROHIBITION ON SOLID BLACK**: Black is strictly avoided at Hindu wedding ceremonies.
  - **PROHIBITION ON PLAIN WHITE**: Solid plain white is associated with widowhood/mourning and is strictly avoided by wedding guests.

## 4. East Asian Traditions (东亚礼仪 / 東アジアの儀礼)

### 4.1 Funerals & Memorials (葬礼 / お葬式 / 장례식)
- **Occasion Type**: Chinese, Japanese, Korean funerals and ancestor memorials.
- **Formality**: Formal dark mourning.
- **Color Palette**: Solid black suit, white shirt, solid plain black tie (men); plain black formal dress or dark kimono (women).
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION ON RED OR GOLD**: In Chinese and East Asian culture, red and gold represent extreme celebration and joy; wearing them to a funeral is considered an unforgivable insult.
  - NO colorful ties, shiny metals, or loud accessories.

### 4.2 Weddings & Banquets (婚礼 / 結婚披露宴)
- **Color Palette**: Refined festive colors (pastels, navy, grey, blush, jewel tones).
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **NO SOLID RED FOR GUESTS**: At Chinese weddings, red is reserved exclusively for the bride.
  - **NO SOLID WHITE FOR GUESTS**: Western and modern East Asian weddings reserve solid white exclusively for the bride.

## 5. Western Ceremonial & Formal Standards

### 5.1 Black Tie & Gala Events
- **Occasion Type**: Charity galas, opera openings, formal evening receptions.
- **Requirements**:
  - **Men**: Black or midnight blue tuxedo (dinner jacket) with satin or grosgrain lapels, matching trousers with side stripe, white pique or pleated tuxedo shirt, black silk bow tie, cummerbund or low-cut waistcoat, black patent leather dress shoes.
  - **Women**: Floor-length formal evening gown, refined dressy cocktail jewelry, evening clutch.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO standard business suits or daytime lounge jackets.
  - NO long ties or neckties (bow tie strictly mandatory for men).
  - NO sneakers, loafers, or open casual sandals.

### 5.2 Western Wedding Guest Etiquette
- **Occasion Type**: Church, chapel, vineyard, or ballroom wedding.
- **Requirements**: Semi-formal to smart-formal; midi or cocktail dresses; tailored suits in navy, charcoal, or seasonal tones.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **NO SOLID WHITE, IVORY, OR CHAMPAGNE**: Reserved strictly for the bride.
  - NO casual denim, t-shirts, sneakers, or athletic wear.

## 6. Implementation & System Integration Architecture

### Ground-Truth RAG Extraction
1. Every section above corresponds to a unique axiom record in `backend/app/data/fashion_rules_seed.json`.
2. The dynamic retriever in `fashion_rules_rag.py` triggers on user prompts containing multilingual terms (Hebrew, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Chinese, English, German, French, etc.).
3. The Stylist QA Engine (`stylist_qa_engine.py`) enforces strict negative constraints in Python code—automatically dropping prohibited colors (e.g. red at Shiva or black at Hindu funerals) and normalizing idioms before display.
