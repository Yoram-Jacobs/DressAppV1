# Creating a New Campaign (Expert Guide)

Formulate and submit local promotions to nearby shoppers.

## Overview

Promote your fashion services, seasonal sales, or stylist packages directly to users near your business. Creating a campaign takes only a few minutes using a simple 6-step setup wizard.

## Prerequisites

* A verified Fashion Expert profile.
* Professional tier subscription.
* A connected **PayPal Account** (Go to **Profile → Payment Settings** and save your PayPal email address).
* A flat campaign fee of **$1.00 USD per day** is charged upon submission.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Start the Wizard

Go to the **Experts** page, switch to the **Campaigns** tab, and click the **New Campaign** button (or go to **Profile → My Campaigns → Create**).

### Step 2: Fill in the 6 Steps

1. **Basic Info**: Enter your Campaign Title (e.g., "25% Off Bridal Alterations"), Business Name, category (e.g., Tailor, Boutique, Stylist), a short summary, and a cover photo URL.
2. **Promotion Details**: Set the discount percentage (optional), coupon code (e.g., "SPRING25"), start date, and end date. Toggle "Limited Time Offer" if it's a flash sale.
3. **Location**: Enter your country (e.g., IL, US), city, and set your **Reach Radius** (5km to 100km). This determines how far around your business the promotion will be shown to users.
4. **Target Audience**: Choose who should see the campaign (Women, Men, Kids, Casual, Luxury, or Sustainable Fashion) and set the age range.
5. **Notifications**: Choose if you want to notify matching users in your area. You can send push notifications, email alerts, or both, immediately upon approval or on the start date.
6. **Review \& Price**: Check all details. The app will calculate your fee: **$1.00 USD per day** between your start and end dates.

### Step 3: Complete Payment \& Submit

1. Click **Submit for Approval**.
2. If your PayPal is unconnected, the app will show a warning link. Connect it in settings.
3. If connected, a yellow PayPal payment box will load. Log in and confirm the payment.
4. Once paid, the campaign status changes to **Pending Approval**, and an automated email alert is sent to the DressApp admin team.

## Expected Results

* After admin approval (usually within 24 hours), your campaign status becomes **Active**.
* Your promotion starts scrolling in the **Ad Ticker** on the Home screen and appears in the **Campaigns** list.
* Push or email alerts are automatically sent to users within your selected radius.

## Troubleshooting

* **Cannot submit — PayPal unconnected**: The app requires a PayPal account to authorize the daily campaign charge. Go to **Profile → Payment Settings** (gear icon) and add your PayPal email address first.
* **Campaign rejected**: If your promotion contains inappropriate text or is not fashion-related, the admin may reject it. You will see a rejection reason in your dashboard. Simply click the gear icon, edit the fields, and resubmit (re-authorization of payment is required).
* **Notification settings disabled**: Push alerts require users to have enabled browser notification permissions. We suggest enabling both Email and Push to reach the maximum number of shoppers.
