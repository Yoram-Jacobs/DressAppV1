# What is DressApp?

Welcome to **DressApp** — your smart digital wardrobe and personal style assistant. DressApp combines intelligent photo recognition, a voice-enabled AI Stylist, and practical wardrobe planning tools to make getting dressed effortless every day.

## Key Features

- **Ready-to-Use AI Styling & Photo Recognition**: Free accounts work right out of the box using **DressApp's on-premises AI model**, while **Manager** ($10/mo) and **Professional** ($15/mo) plans unlock our faster **cutting-edge AI model** (with automatic fallback to our on-premises AI model so your styling sessions are never interrupted).
- **Effortless Wardrobe Digitization & Cluster Upload**: Photograph a single piece of clothing or a full outfit you are wearing. On **Manager** and **Professional** plans, you can also use **Cluster Garment Uploading** to photograph garments hanging in your closet or on a hanger, a rack of shoes, or a pile of shirts—using fewer photos to digitize your physical closet in no time.
- **Voice-Enabled AI Stylist**: Speak naturally with your personal stylist to get outfit suggestions tailored to your closet, local weather, calendar events, and personal taste.
- **Virtual Try-On & Outfit Planner**: Mix, match, and layer your clothes on your realistic 3D dynamic avatar (or personal full-body photo) before getting dressed.
- **Travel Suitcase Assistant**: Generate complete day-by-day packing lists tailored to your destination's weather and trip itinerary.
- **Wardrobe Insights**: Track your closet's total value, cost per wear, most-worn items, and color distribution.
- **Community Marketplace**: Buy, sell, rent, swap, or donate pre-loved clothes, and test how marketplace items look with your own wardrobe before buying.
- **Trend Scout & Professional Stylists**: Explore daily fashion inspiration matched to your closet, or connect with certified human stylists for one-on-one consultations.
- **60-Second Personalized Onboarding & Starter Capsule**: Calibrate your style DNA, 3D/2D avatar measurements, and select from 48 pre-cataloged womenswear and menswear staples (covering trousers, skirts, dresses, abayas, kaftans, jalabiyas, sarees, thobes, kurtas, and tailored suits) to assemble your first weather-aware AI outfit before signing in.
