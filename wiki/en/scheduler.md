# Morning Schedule & Push Alerts

Automate your morning style choices with cron outfit alerts.

## Overview
The Wardrobe Scheduler delivers custom morning push notifications containing three styled outfit layouts. It coordinates weather updates and calendar events to keep your look fresh and prevent repeat wear patterns.

## Prerequisites
- Push notification permissions allowed in your device browser.
- Stored items in your closet (at least one top, bottom, and footwear).
- Custom Gemini API keys (recommended).

## Step-by-Step
1. **Enable Alerts**: Go to Profile details -> Scheduler & Push. Toggle **Enable Notifications**.
2. **Set Timing**: Choose what time you want to receive your daily alert (e.g., 07:30 AM).
3. **Calendar Sync**: Link Google Calendar so the system knows your daily activities.
4. **Receive suggestion**: Tap the notification to open the outfit list.
5. **Confirm Wear**: Select one layout to save it to your daily diary.

## Expected Results
A push notification arriving at your scheduled time showing optimized style options.

## Troubleshooting
- **No notifications arriving**: Check if your browser/OS has muted notification permissions for DressApp.
- **Repeat outfits suggested**: Populate your wear diary inside the calendar; the rotation algorithm requires history logs to filter repeats.

## Limitations
- Notifications require a stable background internet connection on the server.
