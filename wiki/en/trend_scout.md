# Trend Scout

**Trend Scout** keeps your wardrobe inspired with a daily curated feed of fashion trends, style news, and outfit ideas tailored to your personal taste.

## Key Features

1. **Personalized Daily Fashion Feed**:
   - Available on the **Manager** ($10/mo) and **Professional** ($15/mo) plans (with a preview available on the Free plan) and powered by our **cutting-edge AI model**, Trend Scout selects articles and looks that match your style preferences and lifestyle.

2. **Four Curated Channels**:
   - **Runway Highlights**: The latest seasonal looks from major fashion weeks.
   - **Style News**: Breaking fashion stories and seasonal guides.
   - **Sustainable Fashion**: Tips on eco-friendly wardrobe care, upcycling, and circular fashion.
   - **Creator Inspiration**: Trending everyday outfits from style creators.

3. **One-Tap "Style with My Closet"**:
   - See a trend you love? Tap **Style with My Closet** on any card, and your AI Stylist will recreate the look using clothes you already own!
