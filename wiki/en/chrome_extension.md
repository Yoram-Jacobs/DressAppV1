# Shopping Assistant Extension

Shop smart online and find the exact sizing matching your physical body measurements.

## Overview
The DressApp Shopping Assistant is a browser extension that integrates with partner retail websites (like Zara and ASOS). It reads size tables dynamically on garment pages, compares them with your profile measurements, and recommends your best fit instantly.

## Prerequisites
- A desktop Google Chrome browser.
- Stored body metrics (shoulder, chest, hips, waist) in your Profile settings.
- Active DressApp user account credentials.

## Step-by-Step
1. **Installation**: Download and install the DressApp extension from the Chrome Web Store.
2. **Connect**: Click the extension icon and log in with your DressApp credentials.
3. **Browse**: Go to a partner store (e.g. Zara) and open a product page.
4. **Compare**: The extension reads the garment sizes and highlights your match (e.g. "Size Large recommended for a relaxed chest fit").
5. **Import**: Tap **Import to Closet** inside the extension popup to save a draft card of the item.

## Expected Results
Accurate, real-time size recommendations rendered directly on retail store product descriptions, eliminating returns due to sizing issues.

## Troubleshooting
- **No recommendation badge**: Ensure you are logged into your account in the extension popup.
- **Measurements blank**: Go back to your mobile app profile details and populate all measurement fields.

## Limitations
- Size chart analysis is limited to supported partner stores.
