# Group Tagging (Bulk Wardrobe Organization)

Organize dozens of garments in seconds with 1-tap batch editing.

## Overview
Group Tagging lets you organize multiple clothes simultaneously instead of editing them one by one. Select multiple items in your closet and assign seasons, formality levels, or custom event tags in a single tap!

## Prerequisites
- Digitized clothes in your closet.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Open Closet**: Navigate to your **Closet** tab.
2. **Enter Select Mode**: Tap the **Select / Batch Edit** button in the header toolbar.
3. **Choose Clothes**: Tap the items you want to tag, or tap a category pill (like *"Select all Tops"*).
4. **Open Group Tag Sheet**: Tap the floating **Group Tag** button at the bottom of the screen.
5. **Set Attributes**:
   - Check the **Seasons** (*Spring*, *Summer*, *Fall*, *Winter*).
   - Pick the **Dress Code** (*Casual*, *Smart Casual*, *Business*, *Formal*, *Athletic*, *Loungewear*).
   - Add **Custom Labels** (like *"Vacation"*, *"Summer Capsule"*, or *"Gym"*).
6. **Apply**: Tap **Apply to Selected Items**. Your entire closet grid updates instantly!

## Expected Results
All selected garments reflect their new tags immediately without page reloads or waiting spinners.

## Troubleshooting
- **Tags didn't apply to an item**: Make sure the item's checkbox was highlighted before clicking Apply.
- **Want to remove a tag**: Select the garments again, uncheck the tag in the dialog, and tap Apply.

## Limitations
- Bulk editing updates tags, seasons, and formality; individual garment titles, brands, and purchase prices are customized inside each item's detail view.
