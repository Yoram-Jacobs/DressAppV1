## Global Quick-Reference Guardrails (Universal Baseline)
Keywords: global, universal, general, international, travel, airport, temple, mosque, church, synagogue, camouflage, modesty

* **Camouflage & Military Apparel Ban**: Never pack camouflage-patterned clothing, hats, shoes, or backpacks when traveling to **Caribbean nations** (Barbados, Jamaica, Trinidad & Tobago, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent & the Grenadines, Antigua & Barbuda, Grenada, Dominica, Saint Kitts & Nevis, Bahamas, Guyana), **African nations** (Nigeria, Uganda, Zimbabwe, Zambia, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Rwanda), or **Philippines, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Colombia, Haiti, and Mexico**. It is a strict-liability criminal offense or paramilitary target.
* **State-Mandated Religious Modesty**: Strict legal dress codes apply in **Iran** (mandatory headscarf & loose manteau for women; no shorts for men), **Afghanistan** (mandatory full hijab/chadori/abaya for women; no shorts or Western ties for men), **Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, UAE (especially Sharjah), Brunei, Aceh (Indonesia), Kelantan & Terengganu (Malaysia), Maldives (inhabited local islands), Yemen, Gaza, Somalia, Sudan, and Mauritania**.
* **Secular Face-Covering Bans**: Full-face veils (*niqab*, *burqa*) and public face-concealing masks/balaclavas are legally banned with criminal/administrative fines in **France, Switzerland, Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Bulgaria, Netherlands (public transport/schools/hospitals/government buildings), and Quebec, Canada (when receiving public services)**.
* **Sacred Precincts Universal Rule**: Always pack at least one lightweight pashmina/scarf, ankle-length loose trousers or maxi skirt, a long-sleeved top with a high neckline, and slip-on shoes with clean non-leather socks when visiting religious sites worldwide.

## Islamic Republic of Iran (Danger Zone — Mandatory Statutory Hijab & Modesty)
Keywords: iran, tehran, isfahan, shiraz, mashhad, qom, yazd, tabriz, kish, persia, איראן, טהרן, إيران, طهران, Иран

### Legal Context & Penalties
* **Governing Law**: **Islamic Penal Code (Book 5, *Ta'zirat*) Article 638 & Note**, plus the **2024 Chastity and Hijab Law** and **FARAJA "Noor Plan" (*Tarh-e Noor*)** AI-assisted surveillance.
* **Penalties**: Appearing in public without mandatory hijab or in "immodest" clothing carries 10 days to 2 months imprisonment, up to 74 lashes, escalated monetary fines (IRR 30M to 1.65B), automatic vehicle impoundment, travel/exit bans, and detention by morality patrols (*Gasht-e Ershad*).

### Women's Dress Code Requirements (All Citizens & Foreign Tourists from Age 9 Lunar)
* **Head Covering**: A headscarf (*rusari* or *shayla*) must cover the hair, neck, and chest at all times in public spaces, streets, hotels, public transport, and from the moment an international flight lands in Iran.
* **Upper Body (*Manteau* / *Roopoosh*)**: A loose-fitting coat, kimono-cut cardigan, or long tunic must cover the torso, arms (to the wrists or lower forearms), and extend down to at least mid-thigh or knee to conceal body contours.
* **Lower Body**: Legs must be covered to the ankles with loose trousers, non-ripped jeans, or a maxi skirt with opaque stockings. Avoid tight-fitting leggings, sheer fabrics, or ripped denim.
* **Holy Shrines (Mashhad Imam Reza, Qom Fatima Masumeh, Shiraz Shah Cheragh)**: Standard street *manteau* + headscarf is insufficient; all women must wear a full-length **Chador** over their clothes (loaned at shrine entrances).

### Men's Dress Code Requirements
* **Lower Body**: Men must wear full-length trousers in all public spaces. **Shorts above or at the knee are illegal and culturally unacceptable in public streets.**
* **Upper Body & Ties**: Sleeveless shirts (tank tops/singlets) are forbidden in public. Western neckties (*kravat*) are restricted in government ministries, courts, and state institutions.

## Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan (Danger Zone — Strict Taliban Virtue & Vice Law)
Keywords: afghanistan, kabul, herat, kandahar, mazar-i-sharif, bamiyan, אפגניסטן, קאבול, أفغانستان, كابول, Афганистан

### Legal Context & Penalties
* **Governing Law**: **Law on the Propagation of Virtue and the Prevention of Vice** (Official Gazette No. 1452, August 21, 2024, Articles 13, 14, 24), enforced by *Muhtasibin* (morality inspectors).
* **Penalties**: Arbitrary checkpoint detention (1–3 days in public jail), confiscation of property, fines, and referral to Taliban Sharia courts for corporal punishment (flogging).

### Women's Dress Code Requirements (Article 13)
* **Full-Body & Face Covering**: Article 13 classifies a woman's entire body as *awrah* in public. Local women are required to wear a full-body *chadori* (blue pleated burqa) or black *abaya* with *niqab* covering the face. Foreign female visitors and humanitarian personnel must wear a loose headscarf covering all hair and neck, a long loose *abaya* or tunic reaching below the knees over loose ankle-length trousers, and carry a face veil for checkpoints.
* **Prohibited Garments**: Any clothing that is **thin/sheer, tight-fitting, or short** (Art. 13(3)), bright/ostentatious colors at checkpoints, or traveling without a male guardian (*mahram*) permit where required.

### Men's Dress Code Requirements (Article 14)
* **Male *Awrah***: Navel to and including the knee must be strictly covered at all times, including during sports. **Shorts and sleeveless tops are strictly banned.**
* **Traditional Attire & Grooming**: Traditional loose **Perahan Tunban** (*shalwar kameez*) with a skullcap (*kufi*) or turban (*lungi*) is strongly recommended and mandatory in government offices. **Western neckties are banned** under Article 14 (deemed a cross symbol). Shaving the beard shorter than one fist-length (*qabzah*) or having a Western undercut/fade haircut is prohibited for residents.

## Kingdom of Saudi Arabia & Haramain Sacred Precincts (Modesty Zone & Holy Places)
Keywords: saudi arabia, riyadh, jeddah, mecca, makkah, medina, madinah, alula, dammam, red sea, ערב הסעודית, סעודיה, מכה, מדינה, ריאד, ג'דה, السعودية, الرياض, جدة, مكة, المدينة, Саудовская Аравия

### Legal Context & Public Decency Regulation
* **Governing Law**: **Saudi Public Decency Charter / Regulation (*La'ihat al-Dhawq al-'Amm*)**, Cabinet Decision No. 444 (September 2019).
* **General Public Spaces (Riyadh, Jeddah, AlUla, Dammam)**:
  * **Abaya & Headscarf**: The traditional *abaya* and headscarf are no longer legally required for foreign female tourists in general public spaces, though modest loose clothing is mandatory.
  * **Shoulder-to-Knee Coverage (Men & Women)**: Both men and women must cover **shoulders and knees** in all public streets, malls, souqs, restaurants, and metro stations (Violation #19: **SAR 100–200** fine).
  * **Prohibited Garments**: Tight-fitting lycra/bodycon clothing, sheer/transparent fabrics, crop tops, shorts above the knee, profanity/political/religious/alcohol graphics on shirts (Violation #10: **SAR 500–1,000** fine), and military camouflage (strictly restricted to armed forces).
  * **Red Sea Ultra-Luxury Private Resorts (e.g., Sindalah, Ummahat Islands)**: Western swimwear/bikinis are permitted on private resort island beaches, but mainland Public Decency rules apply at airports and cities.

### Mecca (*Makkah*) & Medina (*Al-Madinah*) — Haramain Sacred Rules
* **Strict Non-Muslim Entry Ban**: Non-Muslims are prohibited by Saudi law from entering the **Haram boundary of Mecca** and the sacred interior plaza of **Al-Masjid an-Nabawi (Prophet's Mosque)** in Medina. Violation leads to arrest, deportation, and permanent re-entry ban.
* **Ihram Pilgrimage Attire (Hajj & Umrah)**:
  * **Men in Ihram**: Must wear two unstitched, unhemmed white cotton/terrycloth sheets (**Izar** wrapped around the waist and **Rida** draped over the shoulders) plus open sandals leaving the ankle bone and upper foot bridge uncovered. **Strictly forbidden for men in Ihram**: Any stitched clothing (shirts, trousers, underwear/briefs, socks, jackets) or any head covering.
  * **Women in Ihram**: Must wear full-length, loose, opaque modest clothing (typically a black or white *abaya* and *hijab*) covering the entire body except the face and hands. **Strictly forbidden for women in Ihram**: The *niqab* (face veil) and gloves.

## Brunei Darussalam (Modesty Zone — Syariah Penal Code)
Keywords: brunei, bandar seri begawan, ברוניי, بروناي, Бруней

### Legal Context & Penalties
* **Governing Law**: **Syariah Penal Code Order, 2013 (SPCO)** (enacted in full April 2019), applying to both Muslims and non-Muslims for public-order offenses.
* **Section 197 (Indecent Dress/Behaviour in Public)**: Wearing revealing, tight, or provocative clothing in public carries a fine up to **BND $2,000**, imprisonment up to **6 months**, or both.
* **Section 198 (Cross-Dressing Ban)**: Strictly criminalizes any man dressing as a woman or any woman dressing as a man in a public place (fine up to **BND $1,000–$4,000** and **3 months to 1 year imprisonment**).
* **Required Attire & Mosque Etiquette**: Cover shoulders to below the knees in all public spaces. When visiting the **Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque** or **Jame' Asr Hassanil Bolkiah Mosque**, visitors must wear the provided full-length black robe (*jubah*) and women must cover their hair. Note that all shops, restaurants, and attractions close by law on **Fridays from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM** (Section 194).

## Indonesia: Aceh Province (Sharia Law), Bali (Hindu Temple Law) & National Anti-Communism Symbol Ban
Keywords: indonesia, aceh, banda aceh, sabang, pulau weh, bali, ubud, uluwatu, besakih, jakarta, yogyakarta, אינדונזיה, באלי, אצ'ה, إندونيسيا, بالي, آتشيه, Индонезия, Бали

### 1. Aceh Province (Special Autonomous Sharia Region)
* **Governing Law**: **Qanun Aceh No. 11/2002** & **Qanun Aceh No. 6/2014 (*Qanun Jinayat*)**, enforced by the Sharia Police (**Wilayatul Hisbah — WH**).
* **Dress Code**:
  * **Muslim Women**: Mandatory hijab (*jilbab*), loose long-sleeved tunic, and loose ankle-length skirt or trousers. **Tight jeans, leggings, and short sleeves are strictly banned** (WH conducts roadside checkpoints to confiscate tight jeans). In Lhokseumawe, adult women are barred from straddling motorbikes behind male drivers unless dressed in loose modest garments and seated side-saddle.
  * **All Visitors (Including Non-Muslims)**: Must cover shoulders, chest, and knees in all public areas. **Shorts above the knee for men or women are prohibited in public.** On Pulau Weh (Sabang), bikinis are strictly banned on public beaches and permitted only within enclosed private resort beaches.

### 2. Bali Province (Hindu *Pura* Sacred Temple Rules & Governor Circular No. 4/2023)
* **Governing Law**: **Bali Governor Circular No. 4 of 2023** & *Awig-Awig Desa Adat*; desecration prosecuted under **Indonesian Criminal Code (KUHP) Art. 156a** (up to 5 years imprisonment and immediate deportation).
* **Temple (*Pura*) Attire (Besakih, Uluwatu, Tirta Empul, Tanah Lot, Lempuyang)**:
  * Both men and women must wear a **Kamben** (traditional Balinese sarong covering waist to ankle) tied with a **Selendang** (waist sash) and a shirt covering the shoulders (no tank tops or swimwear).
  * **Menstruation Ban**: Under Balinese Hindu *Sebel* purity laws, women who are menstruating or within 42 days postpartum, and anyone with an open bleeding wound, are strictly barred from entering any *Pura*.
  * **Scooter & Village Street Modesty**: Riding scooters shirtless or walking in only a bikini/swim trunks through village streets away from the beach is prohibited.

### 3. Nationwide Indonesian Ban on Communist & Separatist Symbols
* **Governing Law**: **Indonesian Criminal Code (KUHP) Art. 107a / New KUHP Art. 188**.
* **Prohibited Apparel**: Wearing T-shirts, caps, pins, or bags depicting the **Communist Hammer and Sickle (*Palu Arit*)**, Marxist/PKI emblems, or the **West Papuan Morning Star (*Bintang Kejora*)** / RMS separatist flags is a serious criminal offense carrying **4 to 12 years imprisonment**.

## Republic of Maldives (Inhabited Local Islands vs. Private Resort Islands)
Keywords: maldives, male, hulhumale, maafushi, thoddoo, dhigurah, guraidhoo, האיים המלדיביים, מלדיביים, المالديف, Мальдивы

### Legal Context & Two-Zone Dress Code
* **Governing Law**: **Maldives Penal Code (Law No. 9/2014, Section 615 — Public Indecency)** & Ministry of Tourism Regulations.
* **Zone 1 — Private Uninhabited Resort Islands**: Bikinis, speedos, and Western resort wear are freely permitted everywhere on private one-island-one-resort properties.
* **Zone 2 — Capital Malé, Hulhumalé, Airports & All Inhabited Local Islands (e.g., Maafushi, Thoddoo, Dhigurah)**:
  * **Strict Bikini & Swimwear Ban**: Wearing a bikini, two-piece swimsuit, sheer cover-up, or walking shirtless on the streets or public beaches of any inhabited local island is **illegal** and results in police arrest and fines.
  * **Designated "Bikini Beaches"**: On local islands, tourists may wear bikinis **only** inside the fenced, palm-screened "Tourist / Bikini Beach" zone, and must put on a T-shirt and knee-length shorts/skirt before stepping off the sand onto island streets.

## UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Jordan, Egypt, Morocco, Algeria & Tunisia (GCC & MENA Modesty Zones)
Keywords: uae, dubai, abu dhabi, sharjah, qatar, doha, kuwait, oman, muscat, bahrain, manama, jordan, amman, egypt, cairo, luxor, morocco, marrakech, marrakesh, fes, casablanca, rabat, algeria, tunisia, דובאי, אבו דאבי, שארג'ה, קטאר, דוחא, עומאן, בחריין, ירדן, מצרים, קהיר, מרוקו, מרקש, קזבלנקה, פאס, תוניסיה, الإمارات, دبي, أبوظبي, الشارقة, قطر, الكويت, عمان, البحرين, الأردن, مصر, المغرب, مراكش, الجزائر, تونس

### 1. United Arab Emirates (Sharjah vs. Dubai & Abu Dhabi)
* **Sharjah (Strictest Emirate — Public Decency Law)**: Both men and women must cover from **shoulders to below the knees** in all public spaces. Swimwear is prohibited on public beaches outside private hotel beaches; cross-dressing is a criminal offense.
* **Dubai & Abu Dhabi (UAE Federal Crimes and Penalties Code Art. 411)**:
  * Bikinis and swim trunks are restricted strictly to beaches, hotel pools, and waterparks; walking into malls, metro stations, or streets in beachwear or shirtless is illegal (up to **AED 1,000–10,000** fine).
  * **Shopping Malls (Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates, Yas Mall) & Government Offices**: Official entrance signs require covered shoulders and knees (no booty shorts, sheer dresses, or micro-crop tops).
  * **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque (Abu Dhabi)**: Strictest dress-code enforcement in the UAE. Women must cover hair completely with a headscarf, arms to the **wrists**, and legs to the **ankles** in loose, opaque garments (tight leggings, skinny jeans, or ankle-baring pants are rejected at security). Men must wear long trousers and sleeved shirts, and cover any visible tattoos depicting faces, religious figures, or profanity.

### 2. Qatar, Kuwait, Oman & Bahrain
* **Qatar (Penal Code Art. 290 & *Al-Fanar* Code)**: Cover shoulders to knees in Souq Waqif, Katara Cultural Village, museums, malls, and public buildings. Tight lycra leggings, spaghetti straps, and men's short-shorts are denied entry at malls; public indecency carries up to **6 months imprisonment or QAR 3,000 fine**.
* **Kuwait (Penal Code Art. 198)**: Strict public modesty in malls and streets; **Art. 198 criminalizes "imitating the appearance of the opposite sex"** (cross-dressing) with up to **1 year imprisonment or KWD 1,000 fine**. Bikinis are banned on public beaches.
* **Oman (Omani Penal Code Art. 265 & 266)**: Cover shoulders and knees in all souqs, malls, and **even when swimming in public wadis** (e.g., Wadi Shab, Bimmah Sinkhole — women and men must wear a T-shirt and knee-length shorts over swimwear in public wadis). **Camouflage clothing is banned** and confiscated at Muscat Airport. Cross-dressing carries up to 1 year imprisonment (Art. 266). At **Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque**, women must cover hair, wrists, and ankles; men must wear full-length trousers.
* **Bahrain (Penal Code Art. 350)**: Cover shoulders and knees in traditional souqs (Muharraq, Manama Souq), villages, and mosques.

### 3. Morocco, Egypt, Jordan, Algeria & Tunisia (Medina, Souq & Mosque Etiquette)
* **Penal Codes**: Jordan (Art. 320), Egypt (Art. 278), Morocco (Art. 483), Algeria (Art. 333), and Tunisia (Art. 226) all penalize public indecency.
* **Historic Medinas, Rural Villages & Archaeological Sites (Marrakech, Fes, Cairo, Luxor, Amman, Petra)**:
  * **Women**: Loose, breathable clothing covering shoulders, cleavage, and knees (linen trousers, midi/maxi skirts, kaftans, or tunics) prevents street harassment and shows cultural respect. Carry a lightweight scarf/pashmina for entering sacred sites.
  * **Men**: Long breathable trousers (linen or cotton chinos) and collared or short-sleeved shirts; avoid walking shirtless or wearing sleeveless tank tops in medinas.
* **Mosque Visitation (Hassan II Mosque in Casablanca, Al-Azhar & Muhammad Ali Mosques in Cairo, King Abdullah I Mosque in Amman)**: Both men and women must cover shoulders, upper arms, and legs to the ankles; women must cover their hair; shoes must be removed before stepping onto prayer carpets.
* **Safety & Security Warnings (Medinas & Crowded Souqs)**:
  * Beware of pickpocketing, bag snatching, and distraction scams in crowded souqs (e.g. Jemaa el-Fnaa in Marrakech, Khan el-Khalili in Cairo).
  * Avoid unlit or deserted narrow alleys (*derbs*) alone late at night; keep valuables in zipped front pockets or cross-body bags.

## Conflict, Sub-National Sharia & Strict Regional Modesty Zones (Yemen, Gaza, Somalia, Sudan, Mauritania, Pakistan, Malaysia, Iraq)
Keywords: yemen, sanaa, aden, gaza, somalia, mogadishu, hargeisa, sudan, khartoum, mauritania, nouakchott, pakistan, karachi, lahore, islamabad, peshawar, kpk, balochistan, malaysia, kuala lumpur, kelantan, terengganu, iraq, baghdad, karbala, najaf, תימן, עזה, סומליה, סודן, פקיסטן, מלזיה, עיראק, اليمن, غزة, الصومال, السودان, موريتانيا, باكستان, ماليزيا, العراق

* **Yemen (Sana'a & Houthi-Controlled Northern Yemen)**: Under Houthi *Mudawwana* directives and *Zaynabiyat* female morality police, women must wear a loose, plain **black abaya** and headscarf/face veil (*lithma*); colored or belted abayas are targeted. Men are barred from wearing shorts.
* **Somalia (Mogadishu, Somaliland & Rural Zones)**: Women in urban areas wear full-length *dirac* with *garbasaar* or loose *jilbab*+*hijab* (women wearing trousers in public streets risk arrest or harassment). In Al-Shabaab rural territories, women are forced to wear heavy thick *jilbabs* and men must wear trousers above the ankle (*isbal*) and are punished for wearing shorts or Western sports jerseys.
* **Sudan & Mauritania**: In Mauritania, women wear the full-body wraparound **Melahfa** and men wear the **Draa** or long trousers; shorts are strictly taboo for both sexes. In Sudan, military checkpoints enforce long loose skirts/abayas and headscarves for women and ban men's shorts.
* **Pakistan (Urban vs. Rural KPK & Balochistan)**: For both men and women nationwide, the **Shalwar Kameez** (loose tunic and trousers, with a **Dupatta** scarf over the chest/head for women) is the safest and most respected daily attire. Adult men never wear shorts in public streets. In Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Peshawar, Swat) and Balochistan, women must wear a full *chador*, *abaya*+*niqab*, or *burqa* outside.
* **Malaysia (Kelantan & Terengganu State Sharia Enactments + Federal Government Buildings)**:
  * In **Kelantan** (Syariah Enactment 2019 s. 29) and **Terengganu** (s. 34/35), Muslim men and women must cover the *aurat* in public (including a ban on shorts above the knee for men), and municipal authorities fine residents for "indecent attire."
  * **Nationwide Government Buildings, Courts, Police Stations & Hospitals**: Strictly ban entry to anyone (male or female, tourist or citizen) wearing shorts, skirts above the knee, sleeveless tops, or flip-flops.
* **Iraq — Holy Shia Shrine Cities (Karbala, Najaf, Kadhimiya, Samarra)**: Women entering the **Imam Husayn**, **Al-Abbas**, or **Imam Ali** shrines must wear a full-length black **Iraqi Abaya (*Abaya Zaynabiya*)** covering from head to toe over modest clothes, with zero makeup. Men must wear long trousers (no shorts). During **Muharram / Ashura / Arba'een**, wear **solid black mourning attire** and never wear red or bright festive colors.

## Statutory Bans on Camouflage & Military Apparel (Caribbean, Africa, Asia & Middle East)
Keywords: barbados, jamaica, trinidad, tobago, saint lucia, st lucia, saint vincent, grenada, antigua, barbuda, dominica, st kitts, nevis, bahamas, guyana, nigeria, lagos, abuja, uganda, kampala, zimbabwe, harare, zambia, lusaka, ghana, accra, kenya, nairobi, south africa, rwanda, kigali, philippines, manila, camouflage, camo, ברבדוס, ג'מייקה, טרינידד, בהאמה, ניגריה, אוגנדה, זימבבואה, זמביה, גאנה, קניה, דרום אפריקה, רואנדה, פיליפינים, הסוואה, מנומר צבאי, جامايكا, نيجيريا, أوغندا, كينيا, الفلبين

### Strict-Liability Criminal Offense Warning
In all of the jurisdictions below, **civilians (including foreign tourists and children) are prohibited by law from importing, possessing, or wearing ANY camouflage-patterned item**—including camo shorts, T-shirts, cargo pants, baseball caps, backpacks, fanny packs, Crocs/shoes, or pink/blue fashion camo:

1. **Caribbean Nations (Airport Customs & Police Confiscation + Fines/Arrest)**:
   * **Barbados** (*Defence Act Cap. 159 s. 188* — up to **BBD $2,000 fine** and **1 year imprisonment**).
   * **Jamaica** (*Defence Act s. 193* — immediate police/customs confiscation and fine).
   * **Trinidad & Tobago** (*Defence Act Chap. 14:01 s. 203 & Customs Act Chap. 78:01* — prohibited import; mandatory confiscation and arrest).
   * **Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent & the Grenadines, Antigua & Barbuda** (*Defence Act 2006 s. 196* — up to **XCD $2,000 fine** or **1 year prison**), **Grenada, Dominica, Saint Kitts & Nevis, The Bahamas** (*Defence Act Ch. 211 s. 193*), and **Guyana** (*Defence Act s. 196*).
2. **African Nations (Military Court Martial, Arrest & Roadside Confiscation)**:
   * **Nigeria** (*Criminal Code Act s. 110(1) & Armed Forces Act s. 251* — military checkpoints subject civilians in camo or jungle hats to immediate stripping, confiscation, and arrest).
   * **Uganda** (*UPDF Act 2005 s. 160 & 2019 Gazette* — bans all camouflage, military fatigues, AND **specifically bans wearing a Red Beret**, punishable by trial before a Military Court Martial and up to **7 years to life imprisonment**).
   * **Zimbabwe** (*Defence Act [Cap. 11:02] s. 99* — roadblock arrest and up to **1 year prison** even for hunting camo).
   * **Zambia** (*Defence Act Cap. 106 s. 192*), **Ghana** (*NLCD 177*), **Kenya** (*Kenya Defence Forces Act 2012 s. 279* — up to **KES 1,000,000 fine** or **5 years prison**), **South Africa** (*Defence Act 42 of 2002 s. 104(5)*), and **Rwanda**.
3. **Asia & Middle East**:
   * **Philippines** (*Revised Penal Code Art. 179 & Republic Act No. 493* — bans wearing AFP/PNP military or police camouflage uniforms/insignia).
   * **Oman & Saudi Arabia** (customs confiscation and arrest for impersonating security forces).

## Authoritarian, Socialist & State-Identity Sartorial Regimes (North Korea, Turkmenistan, Bhutan, China, Chechnya, Russia)
Keywords: north korea, dprk, pyongyang, turkmenistan, ashgabat, bhutan, thimphu, paro, china, xinjiang, urumqi, kashgar, beijing, shanghai, chechnya, grozny, russia, moscow, צפון קוריאה, טורקמניסטן, בהוטן, סין, צ'צ'ניה, רוסיה, كوريا الشمالية, تركمانستان, بوتان, الصين, الشيشان, روسيا

### 1. North Korea (DPRK — *Reactionary Ideology Rejection Law* & Monument Protocol)
* **Strict Bans**:
  * **Blue Jeans, Skinny Jeans & Ripped Denim**: Banned for citizens as symbols of "American capitalist imperialism" and strongly discouraged for tourists (strictly barred at state monuments).
  * **Leather Trench Coats**: Reserved for Supreme Leader Kim Jong-un's image; civilians are prohibited from wearing imitative leather trench coats.
  * **Western/South Korean Slogans & Flags**: Never wear clothing printed with US, South Korean, or Japanese flags, political English slogans, or K-pop styling.
* **Mandatory Formal Attire for Foreign Tourists at State Mausoleums**:
  * To enter the **Kumsusan Palace of the Sun** or bow at the **Mansudae Grand Monument**, foreign tourists must wear **strict formal attire**: collared dress shirt, tie/jacket, tailored dress trousers, and closed dress shoes for men; modest collared blouse, tailored trousers or below-knee skirt, and closed-toe shoes for women (**zero jeans, T-shirts, shorts, sandals, hats, or sunglasses**).

### 2. Turkmenistan (Ashgabat State Modesty & Grooming Decrees)
* **Women**: Local women in state roles/universities must wear floor-length embroidered **Koynek** dresses and head coverings (**Yaglyk** / **Tahya**). Tight-fitting dresses, jeans, shorts, above-the-ankle skirts, bleached blonde hair, and heavy cosmetic enhancements are targeted by police fines in Ashgabat. Foreign female travelers should wear loose midi/maxi skirts or loose trousers covering shoulders and knees.
* **Men**: **Wearing shorts in public streets, parks, or government offices in Ashgabat is prohibited.** Young men with long untrimmed beards face security questioning.

## Sikh Gurdwaras, Bahá'í Shrines & Indigenous Sacred Precincts
Keywords: amritsar, golden temple, harmandir sahib, punjab, sikh, gurdwara, gurudwara, haifa, akko, acre, bahai, baha'i, new zealand, maori, marae, hopi, uluru, amish, אמריטסר, מקדש הזהב, סיקים, גורדווארה, חיפה, עכו, הגנים הבהאיים, ניו זילנד, מאורים, المعبد الذهبي, السيخ, حيفا, عكا, البهائيين

* **Sikh Gurdwaras Worldwide (Harmandir Sahib / Golden Temple in Amritsar & All Gurdwaras)**:
  1. **Mandatory Head Covering (All Genders)**: Every visitor must cover their head with a tied cloth scarf (**Rumāl**, **Chunni/Dupatta**, or **Dastar** turban)—free cloth scarves are provided at the gate. **Baseball caps, sunhats, or visors are strictly prohibited.**
  2. **Barefoot & Foot Washing**: Remove both **shoes AND socks** at the *Jora Ghar* and walk through the shallow water pool (**Charan Ganga**) to wash feet before entering.
  3. **Modesty**: Cover shoulders to knees/ankles in loose clothing suitable for sitting cross-legged on the floor (never point feet toward the holy scripture).
  4. **Absolute Ban on Tobacco & Intoxicants on Person**: **Never carry cigarettes, vapes, chewing tobacco, lighters, or alcohol in your pockets or bag** inside a Gurdwara complex—this is a severe sacrilege strictly policed by *Sewadar* guards.
* **Bahá'í World Centre Shrines & Terraced Gardens (Haifa & Akko/Bahjí, Israel)**:
  * Both men and women must wear clothing covering **shoulders and knees** (no shorts above the knee, no sleeveless tank tops, no ripped jeans exposing upper legs) to enter the terraced gardens, and remove shoes inside the Shrines.
* **Māori *Marae* (New Zealand — *Pōwhiri* Welcome Ceremony)**:
  * Visitors (*Manuhiri*) wear conservative **formal black or dark attire** (men in dark trousers and collared shirt/jacket; women in a modest dark skirt/dress covering the knees—traditional *Marae* require skirts rather than trousers for women in the front row). Remove all shoes before entering the carved meeting house (*Wharenui*).
