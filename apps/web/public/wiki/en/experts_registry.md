# Certified Stylist Directory

Collaborate with vetted fashion professionals, tailors, and personal designers.

## Overview
Browse the Experts Directory to hire styling advisors who can access your digital closet, or register your own services to get client leads.

## Prerequisites
- A professional bio and contact details if registering as an expert.

## Step-by-Step
1. **Browse Registry**: Open the Experts tab and search by specialty, city, or country.
2. **Contact Stylists**: View portfolios and click direct links for email, phone, or website contact.
3. **Register Services**: Go to Profile Details, toggle "Fashion Pro", fill in your business name and bio, and click Save.
4. **Rank locally**: Browser geolocation helps local users find you first in their region.

## Expected Results
A detailed, region-aware directory of fashion specialists with secure contact paths.

## Troubleshooting
- **Not visible in directory**: Ensure your profile approval status is "approved" (pending admin review upon registry).
- **Local search incorrect**: Check that your city and country parameters are spelled correctly.

## Limitations
- Styling contracts, fees, and sessions are arranged directly between you and the expert outside the DressApp platform.
