# Travel Suitcase Assistant

The **Suitcase Assistant** takes the stress out of packing by checking your destination's weather forecast, trip duration, and planned activities to build a complete day-by-day packing checklist from your closet.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Enter Your Trip Details**:
   - Open the **Suitcase** page and enter your destination city, travel dates, and the types of activities you have planned (such as business meetings, sightseeing, beach days, or formal dinners).

2. **Review Your Smart Packing Checklist**:
   - Powered by DressApp's AI, the assistant checks the destination's weather forecast and selects versatile clothes from your closet that mix and match across your trip.
   - If rain or cold weather is expected, it reminds you to pack essentials like an umbrella or warm coat.

3. **Refine via Chat**:
   - Want to swap an item or add an extra look? Simply type in the chat box (for example, *"Pack a casual outfit for day 2"*) and the assistant will update your list.

4. **Approve & Pack**:
   - Tap **Approve Suitcase** to save your list, then check off each item as you place it into your bag!
