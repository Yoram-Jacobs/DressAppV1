# Talking to Your AI Stylist

Your **AI Stylist** is a personal fashion advisor available 24/7. It looks at the clothes in your closet, checks your local weather and calendar, and suggests complete outfits tailored to your taste.

## How the AI Works
- **Manager & Professional Plans**: Powered by DressApp's **cutting-edge AI model** for fast, detailed styling advice and outfit composition.
- **Free Plan & Automatic Backup**: Powered by **DressApp's on-premises AI model** with zero setup required. If cloud capacity is ever busy, paid plans also automatically fall back to the on-premises AI model so your conversation continues smoothly without interruption.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open the AI Stylist**:
   - Tap **AI Stylist** in the main navigation menu.

2. **Speak or Type Your Request**:
   - Tap the **Microphone** button and ask your question out loud (for example, *"What should I wear with my beige trousers for a dinner date tonight?"*), or type your message in the chat box.

3. **Get Personalized Outfit Suggestions**:
   - The Stylist checks your local weather forecast, your upcoming calendar events, and your style preferences, then selects matching items directly from your closet.

4. **Listen, Save, or Customize**:
   - Listen to the Stylist's spoken explanation, tap **Play Reply** to hear it again, save the outfit to your collection, or open it in the **Outfit Planner** to swap pieces.
