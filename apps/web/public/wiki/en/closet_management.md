# Managing Your Closet

Your **Closet** page is the central hub where you can browse, filter, organize, and edit all of your digitized clothes.

## Key Features

1. **Browse, Search & Filter**:
   - View all your tops, bottoms, dresses, outerwear, shoes, and accessories in a clean grid.
   - Filter by category, color, season, or occasion, or search naturally by meaning (for example, *"warm winter sweater"* or *"summer wedding guest dress"*).

2. **Select Multiple Items**:
   - Turn on selection mode to choose several items at once—ideal for linking pieces together or deleting items you no longer own.

3. **Matched Sets & Similar Items**:
   - **Matched Sets**: Link pieces that belong together (such as a two-piece suit or matching workout set) so your AI Stylist suggests them together.
   - **Similar Item Groups**: Stack similar items (such as basic T-shirts in different colors) under a single cover card to keep your closet grid tidy.

4. **Edit Item Details**:
   - Tap any item to open its details view, where you can update its category, colors, fabric, season, brand, size, or purchase price, or ask the AI to touch up the photo.
