# Virtual Closet Command Center

Manage, search, and group your digitized wardrobe with ease.

## Overview
The Closet workspace displays your entire inventory in an instant-paint grid, supporting text query filters, semantic similarity search, and coordinated group sets.

## Prerequisites
- Stored closet items (at least one item digitized).

## Step-by-Step
1. **Browse**: Scroll through your grid. A floating Scroll-To-Top button is available for fast returns.
2. **Search**: Enter keywords (e.g. "red shirt") or use semantic search to retrieve matching items instantly.
3. **Bulk Tag/Delete**: Press the selector button to bulk edit tags or remove outdated items.
4. **Groups & Sets**: Drag and drop items onto each other to create multi-view single items or coordinated clothing sets.

## Expected Results
Fast navigation and query execution, with instant UI rendering backed by client-side local caching.

## Troubleshooting
- **Grey/Empty cards**: If items display grey placeholders, click "Repair Thumbnails" in settings to regenerate card previews.
- **Wrong group category**: Split the group back to individual items to reset categorizer gates.

## Limitations
- Semantic search operates best on clear, descriptive queries.
