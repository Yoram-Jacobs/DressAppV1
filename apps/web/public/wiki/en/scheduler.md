# Morning Outfit Alerts & Calendar Sync

Start every morning with a ready-to-wear outfit suggestion tailored to your day's weather forecast and calendar schedule.

## Prerequisites
- An active **Manager** or **Professional** plan (or available styling credits).
- Browser notifications enabled on your device.
- Location services enabled for accurate local weather.

## Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Open Morning Outfit Alerts**:
   - Go to your **Profile** page and scroll to the **Morning Outfit Alerts** section.

2. **Choose Your Schedule**:
   - Turn on daily reminders, select which days of the week you want alerts, and pick your preferred morning delivery time.

3. **Pick Your Daily Style Focus**:
   - Choose the overall vibe for your daily suggestions—such as **Casual**, **Work / Formal**, **Athletic**, or **Custom**.

4. **Connect Your Calendar**:
   - Link your Google Calendar so your AI Stylist knows if you have an important meeting, workout, or dinner party scheduled that day.

5. **View Your Morning Look**:
   - When your morning alert arrives, tap it to see the complete outfit selected from your closet along with the weather forecast and styling notes!
