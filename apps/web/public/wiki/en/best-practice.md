## 2. Garment Photography Best Practices

When you upload a photo, DressApp's AI automatically detects whether your image shows a **single garment**, a **full outfit you are wearing**, or a **cluster of garments** (available on **Manager** and **Professional** plans, as well as to **Free users who possess 15 or more AI reconstruction credits**). Taking clean, well-lit photos ensures accurate cutouts and color recognition on the first try—saving your **Studio Reconstruction Credits** for when you really need them.

### Cluster Garment Uploading (Manager & Professional Plans, or Free Users with 15+ AI Reconstruction Credits)

Cluster garment uploading (mass pipeline) is a revolutionary feature that lets you take photos directly in your physical closet, use far fewer photos, and digitize your wardrobe in no time. It can identify and reconstruct:
- **Garments hanging in the closet or on a hanger**
- **A rack of shoes**
- **A pile of shirts or folded clothes**
- **Multiple garments arranged together** (not just garments spread on a bed!)

> **How Free Users Unlock Cluster Uploading (15+ AI Reconstruction Credits):** Cluster garment uploading uses **Nano Banana** studio AI reconstruction and can process up to 15 garments from a single photo. Free accounts start with **5 one-time onboarding bonus reconstruction credits** to test studio repairs on individual items. A single cluster photo would immediately exhaust these starter bonus credits halfway through. Free users can purchase an **AI credit pack** on the Pricing page and immediately unlock the Cluster pipeline whenever their balance is **15 or more AI reconstruction credits**, or upgrade to a **Manager** or **Professional** plan (which includes 100 monthly Studio Reconstruction credits). Free users with any reconstruction credit balance (> 0) can also use the AddItem re-analyzer. If a Free user with fewer than 15 AI reconstruction credits uploads a cluster photo, DressApp prompts them to **skip the image, top up AI credits, or upgrade their plan**.

**Cluster Uploading Fallbacks & Best Practices:**
1. **Stick to Solid Colors & Simple Patterns**: Cluster upload is great for solid colors or very simple plaid or striped patterns. It cannot analyze hidden prints and fine details—photograph intricate prints individually.
2. **Garment Condition**: The reconstructed studio image rarely reflects the current physical condition or wear of the garment.
3. **Check for Skipped Garments**: It may skip some garments in dense or overlapping clusters.
4. **Watch for Hallucinated Patterns or Colors**: It can occasionally hallucinate patterns and colors on partially hidden pieces—always review the item card before saving.
5. **Mind Your Reconstruction Credits**: Every reconstructed piece consumes reconstruction credits.

> **Plan Demotion Note:** If a Manager or Professional user later demotes to the Free plan, they lose their extra closet slots, **leaving only the last 100 added items in their closet**. DressApp warns you about losing older closet assets and asks for confirmation before completing any planned demotion.

### The 4 Golden Rules of Flat-Lay Photography

1. **Use a Bright Room & a Contrasting Surface**
   - Lay the garment flat in a bright, naturally lit room.
   - Place the garment on a **contrasting-color surface** (for example, use a dark floor or mat for white, cream, or pastel clothes, and a light sheet or surface for black, navy, or dark clothes).
   - Clear contrast around the edges helps the AI trace hems, collars, and straps cleanly without picking up background colors.

2. **Center the Garment Straight with Small Margins**
   - Smooth out folds and wrinkles, and spread sleeves or trouser legs naturally so the full shape is visible (for example, so a long-sleeved hoodie is clearly distinguishable from a short-sleeved T-shirt).
   - If photographing multiple items together, leave a small gap between each piece so they can be separated cleanly.
   - Align the garment **straight in the center of the camera frame**, leaving **small, even margins** around all four edges without cutting off collars, cuffs, or hemlines.

3. **Avoid Shadows Across the Garment**
   - Check your position before taking the photo to make sure neither your body nor your phone casts a shadow across the fabric.
   - Strong shadows can make white look grey or navy blue look black. Indirect daylight near a window or even room lighting works best.

4. **Hold the Camera Steady**
   - Hold your phone parallel to the garment and **stay steady** when pressing the shutter.
   - A sharp, blur-free photo helps DressApp's on-premises AI model recognize fine fabric textures (such as linen vs. cotton, or wool vs. knitwear) and subtle patterns.

## 3. Closet Digitizing: 5-Day Onboarding Plans

Digitizing an entire wardrobe at once can feel overwhelming. By breaking your closet into **5 simple daily categories (10 items per day)**—or photographing one shelf a day and uploading them together on Day 5—you can digitize your **50 core wardrobe essentials in 5 days** using your free On-Premises AI Credits.

### Option A: The "10 Items a Day" Steady Plan (10 Credits / Day)

Upload and review 10 garments each day in just a few minutes:

| Day | Focus Category | Target Items (10 Garments) | Credits Used |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1** | **Everyday Bottoms** | Your 10 most-worn jeans, chinos, tailored trousers, skirts, and shorts | 10 credits |
| **Day 2** | **Everyday Tops & Tees** | 10 essential T-shirts, polos, tank tops, and casual blouses | 10 credits |
| **Day 3** | **Shirts, Knitwear & Dresses** | 10 button-down shirts, hoodies, sweaters, cardigans, or dresses | 10 credits |
| **Day 4** | **Outerwear & Layering Pieces** | 10 blazers, jackets, coats, overshirts, and vests | 10 credits |
| **Day 5** | **Footwear & Accessories** | 6–7 go-to pairs of shoes (sneakers, loafers, boots, heels) + 3–4 belts, bags, or hats | 10 credits |

### Option B: The "Shelf-by-Shelf" Batch Plan (50 Credits on Day 5)

If you prefer to take all your photos first and upload them later, take advantage of **30-day credit accumulation**:

- **Days 1–4 (Photograph & Accumulate):** Spend 5 minutes each day photographing one shelf or category into a dedicated album on your phone. While you shoot, your On-Premises AI Credits automatically accumulate (**10 → 20 → 30 → 40 credits**).
- **Day 5 (Upload All 50 Garments):** On Day 5, your balance reaches **50 On-Premises AI Credits**. Open **Add Item**, select your photos (single items, full outfits, or multi-item layouts), and digitize your entire 50-item core wardrobe in one session!

## 4. Item Editing: Always Review Newly Added Garments

> **Important:** **Your AI Stylist relies directly on accurate item details.** Make it a habit to glance over every newly added item card, check its detected tags, and adjust any detail if needed before saving.

When the AI Stylist builds an outfit or packs a suitcase, it reads the saved details on each item card and checks that every outfit forms a complete, appropriate look for your occasion and weather.

### The 5 Details to Check on Every New Item

1. **Category & Sub-Category** (Tops, Bottoms, Outerwear, Full-Body/Dresses, Footwear, Accessories, Traditional Wear)
   - *Why it matters:* Every complete outfit needs a matching combination of clothing and footwear. If a blazer is saved as a regular shirt instead of Outerwear, or a long-sleeved hoodie is saved as a short-sleeved tee, the Stylist may layer it incorrectly.
2. **Colors** (Primary & Secondary Colors)
   - *Why it matters:* Indoor lighting can occasionally shift navy to black or cream to white. Verifying the color ensures harmonious color combinations and respects event etiquette.
3. **Material / Fabric** (Cotton, Linen, Wool, Denim, Leather, Silk, etc.)
   - *Why it matters:* Helps the Stylist pick breathable fabrics in summer heat, warm layers in winter, and appropriate materials for cultural or religious occasions.
4. **Dress Code / Style** (Casual, Smart-Casual, Business, Formal, Athletic, Festive/Traditional)
   - *Why it matters:* Ensures the Stylist never recommends gym wear or beach shorts for a wedding or business meeting.
5. **Season & Pattern** (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter • Solid, Striped, Plaid, Floral)
   - *Why it matters:* Prevents clashing patterns and keeps heavy winter coats out of warm-weather suggestions.

## 5. Stylist Tips: How to Ask the AI Stylist for the Best Results

Every AI Stylist request on the Free plan uses **1 On-Premises AI Credit**. Knowing how DressApp’s AI Stylist understands your request helps you get the exact outfit or wardrobe advice you want right away.

### How the AI Stylist Understands Your Request

1. **Works in Your Language:** You can chat or speak in any of DressApp’s 13 supported languages.
2. **Two Smart Modes (Outfit Ideas vs. Wardrobe Review):**
   - **Mode 1 — Outfit Ideas ("Dress Me"):** When you ask *what to wear* for an event, the Stylist creates **1–2 complete outfits** using only the clothes inside your digital closet, explaining why the pieces work together.
   - **Mode 2 — Wardrobe Review ("What Am I Missing?"):** When you ask *what basic items am I missing?* or *review my closet*, the Stylist looks at the balance of your wardrobe (formal wear, everyday basics, outerwear, shoes, and accessories) and suggests **5–7 practical pieces** that would unlock the most new combinations.
3. **Context & Etiquette Awareness:**
   - The Stylist automatically considers four key details from your request:
     1. **Occasion / Activity** (e.g., *job interview, wedding guest, date night, office day, weekend walk*)
     2. **Cultural, Religious, or Local Etiquette** (e.g., *Shabbat, synagogue, church visit, mosque, Hindu wedding, temple visit, condolence visit*)
     3. **Weather & Temperature** (e.g., *rainy, chilly 10°C evening, hot sunny afternoon*)
     4. **Specific Item or Color Preference** (e.g., *build around my navy blazer, earth tones, flat shoes*)

### The Simple 4-Part Formula for Great Requests

To get the most accurate recommendation in **1 credit**, include these four details:

> **`[1. What You Need]` + `[2. Occasion & Venue]` + `[3. Weather / Time of Day]` + `[4. Favorite Item or Color Preference]`**

### Example Requests

#### A. Asking for a Complete Outfit
* **Example:** *"Build an outfit for a formal job interview tomorrow morning. It will be cool and rainy (12°C). I prefer navy and white."*
* **Why it works:** Gives the Stylist the exact dress code, weather protection needs, and preferred colors.

#### B. Building Around a Specific Piece in Your Closet
* **Example:** *"What should I wear for a smart-casual dinner tonight with my olive chinos? Warm evening weather."*
* **Why it works:** Locks in your olive trousers as the starting piece and selects a matching shirt, shoes, and accessories.

#### C. Cultural, Religious, or Travel Etiquette
* **Example:** *"Recommend a respectful outfit from my closet for visiting a church in Rome on a sunny afternoon."* (or *"What should I wear to a festive cultural celebration?"*)
* **Why it works:** Automatically applies modesty and cultural guidelines while choosing comfortable clothes for the weather.

#### D. Reviewing Your Closet for Missing Basics
* **Example:** *"Review my closet and tell me which everyday essentials I am missing to create more smart-casual outfits."*
* **Why it works:** Switches the Stylist into Wardrobe Review mode and highlights the most useful additions for your closet.

### Quick Tips to Keep in Mind

* **Avoid one-word messages** like *"Outfit"* or *"Help"*—mentioning where you are going and what the weather is like makes a huge difference.
* **Ask for an outfit and a closet review in separate messages** so the Stylist can give you a complete answer for each.
* **Keep Your Profile Updated:** Save your **Gender**, **Modesty Preference**, and **Home Location** in your Profile—the Stylist uses them automatically every time!
