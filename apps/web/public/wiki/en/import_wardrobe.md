# Import Your Wardrobe - Detailed Guide

## Overview

Already have your wardrobe tracked in another app? No problem! DressApp makes it easy to import your existing wardrobe data so you don't have to start from scratch. We support imports from a wide range of popular wardrobe and outfit planning apps.

## Supported Import Sources

- **Cladwell** - Export your Cladwell wardrobe and import directly into DressApp
- **Stylebook** - Transfer your Stylebook inventory with ease
- **Acloset** - Import your Acloset items and outfits
- **SmartCloset** - Migrate your SmartCloset wardrobe data
- **CSV Files** - Import from any app that supports CSV export (generic format)
- **Other Apps** - Many other wardrobe apps support CSV export, which DressApp can import

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Open the Closet Page
Navigate to your **Closet** page in DressApp. This is where all your imported items will appear.

### Step 2: Access the Import Feature
Look for the **Import** button on the Closet page. It's usually located in the top-right corner or within the menu options.

### Step 3: Select Your Source App
Choose the app you're importing from from the list of supported apps. If your app isn't listed, select **CSV File** for a generic import option.

### Step 4: Export Data from Old App
Follow the instructions provided for your specific app:
- **Cladwell**: Go to Settings > Export Data > Download CSV
- **Stylebook**: Open Menu > Export > Choose CSV format
- **Acloset**: Navigate to Profile > Export Wardrobe > Download
- **SmartCloset**: Go to Settings > Data Management > Export
- **CSV Export**: Look for an export or download option in your app's settings

### Step 5: Upload to DressApp
Upload the exported file to DressApp. The system will automatically:
- Parse the data and map fields to DressApp's format
- Categorize items based on their type (tops, bottoms, dresses, etc.)
- Organize colors and sizes
- Import images if available

### Step 6: Review and Adjust
After the import completes:
- Review your items in the Closet page
- Adjust any categories that may have been incorrectly assigned
- Add any missing details (brand, price, purchase date)
- Delete any duplicates or test items

## What Gets Imported

Depending on the source app, the following data may be imported:
- Item names and descriptions
- Categories and subcategories
- Colors and patterns
- Sizes and measurements
- Brand information
- Purchase dates and prices
- Item images (if available in the export)
- Wearing history (if supported)

## Troubleshooting

### Import Failed
- Check that the file format is correct (CSV, JSON, or app-specific format)
- Ensure the file isn't corrupted or too large
- Try exporting again from the source app

### Items Missing After Import
- Some fields may not have mapped correctly
- Check the Import Results page for any warnings
- Manually add missing items if needed

### Images Not Imported
- Not all apps include images in their export files
- You can manually add images to imported items later
- Use the camera or upload feature on the item details page

## Tips for a Successful Import

1. **Clean Up First**: Remove duplicates and test items from your old app before exporting
2. **Check Categories**: Make sure items are properly categorized in the source app
3. **Verify Data**: Open the exported file to ensure it contains all the expected information
4. **Start Small**: If you have a large wardrobe, consider importing in batches
5. **Backup First**: Keep a copy of your exported file in case you need to re-import

## After Importing

Once your wardrobe is imported:
- **Browse Your Closet**: All your items will appear in the Closet page
- **Create Outfits**: Start mixing and matching imported items
- **Get AI Recommendations**: The AI Stylist will use your imported wardrobe for suggestions
- **Track Usage**: Log when you wear items to build your wearing history
- **Sync Across Devices**: Your imported wardrobe syncs automatically across all your devices
