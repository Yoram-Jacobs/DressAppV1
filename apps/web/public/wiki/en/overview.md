# Welcome to DressApp

Welcome to your ultimate digital wardrobe companion! DressApp is designed to help you digitize, organize, and make the most out of your physical closet.

## Overview
DressApp transforms your physical clothes into a structured digital archive powered by a state-of-the-art multi-tier AI engine:
- **Zero-Friction Intelligent Styling**: Driven by **Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite** as the primary engine for sub-350ms responses, backed by an on-premises **Gemma-4-E4B** container serving as a transparent Quota Safety Net. You get smart recommendations instantly without needing personal API keys.
- **Calendar & Weather Integration**: Real-time morning recommendations tailored to daily weather forecasts and your personal Google Calendar appointments.
- **Outfit Planner & Avatar Canvas**: Layer garments digitally on a personalized 2D vector mannequin or your own photo cutout.
- **Interactive Packing Assistant**: AI-curated suitcase checklists for trips of any length.
- **Shopping Assistant**: Chrome extension and universal bookmarklet for smart size and fit comparisons across online retailers.
- **Circular Marketplace**: Direct listings to sell, swap, or rent unused garments with integrated buyer-seller safety.

## Prerequisites
- A modern web browser (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, or Mozilla Firefox recommended).
- Internet access.
- Access to your device camera or photo library for digitizing clothes.
- *(Optional)* Microphone permission for conversational voice styling.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Digitize**: Capture clothes using your camera, upload photos, or import digital receipts. The app automatically segments items and removes backgrounds.
2. **Organize**: Tag, search, and group garments into collections inside your Closet workspace.
3. **Consult the Stylist**: Tap the AI Stylist to ask for context-aware outfit recommendations via voice or text.
4. **Plan & Layer**: Assemble looks on the Outfit Canvas, review side-by-side layering, and schedule them in your Diary.
5. **Pack for Travel**: Generate tailored suitcase packing lists based on your destination's upcoming weather.
6. **Resell or Swap**: Post unworn garments directly to the circular marketplace to monetize your wardrobe.

## Expected Results
You achieve a structured, accessible digital closet that eliminates morning style fatigue, increases garment utilization, and helps you make sustainable fashion choices.

## Troubleshooting
- **Pages or images slow to load**: Ensure you have a stable network connection; offline cached items remain accessible.
- **Layout direction issues**: Toggle between languages; DressApp automatically mirrors interface alignment for RTL languages (Hebrew, Arabic).

## Limitations
- Closet capacity on the Free Tier starts at 50 items (expandable up to 150 via referral bonuses); upgrading to Manager or Professional unlocks unlimited storage.
- Background removal and segmentation perform best with good lighting and contrasting backgrounds.
