# Local Fashion Campaigns

Discover and interact with regional fashion promotions and expert recommendations.

## Overview
The Campaigns system links you to verified fashion professionals in your neighborhood. Boutiques, tailors, personal shoppers, and stylists publish targeted campaigns showcasing discounts, styling packages, and new store arrivals. As a regular user, you can browse, save, and navigate to these local offers.

## Prerequisites
- A verified DressApp account.
- **Location Services** enabled to find offers near you.

## Step-by-Step
1. **Browse Campaigns**: Open the **Experts** page and click the **Campaigns** tab to see active promotions near your location.
2. **View Details**: Click on any campaign to see its complete description, coupon codes, and run dates.
3. **Locate on Google Maps**: Use the embedded map or click the address link to see the business location on Google Maps and get directions.
4. **Save & Share**: Toggle the bookmark button on any campaign to save it to your dashboard, or click share to send it to friends.
5. **Redeem coupon**: Copy the coupon code listed on the campaign page and present it at the local business.

## Expected Results
- Live local promotions populated in your radius.
- Interactive mapping showing precise business locations.
- Seamless redirection to maps and contact details.

## Troubleshooting
- **No campaigns visible**:
  - Check if your max distance in **Profile → Campaign Notifications** is set too narrow (try widening it to 25km or 50km).
  - Ensure your device location permissions are active.
- **Coupon code invalid**: Verify the campaign's run dates; some offers are only valid during limited times.

## Limitations
- Campaigns are only shown if their targets match your profile age range and styling preferences.
- Offers are strictly local; if there are no verified experts running active promotions in your region, the feed will show a fallback prompt.
