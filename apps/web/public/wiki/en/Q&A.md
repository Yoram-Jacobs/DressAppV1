## 1. Getting Started & Profile Setup

### Q: What is the very first thing I should do after signing up?
**A:** Complete your **Profile** before uploading clothes! Open the **Profile** tab and fill in four essential settings:
1. **Gender Preference**: Ensures newly uploaded garments inherit the right default classification and fit rules.
2. **Modesty Level**: Tells the AI Stylist your personal coverage preferences so every outfit suggestion feels comfortable and authentic to you.
3. **Home Location**: Enables real-time local weather integration so DressApp never suggests a heavy wool coat on a warm, sunny morning.
4. **Body Measurements**: Enter just **4 simple inputs** (Height, Weight, Waist, and Bra/Chest size). DressApp's biometric engine (trained on the ANSUR II anthropometric dataset) automatically predicts **6 additional body measurements** to power accurate size recommendations and silhouette styling.

### Q: How long does it take to digitize my closet?
**A:** You do not need to photograph your entire closet in one afternoon! We recommend the **5-Day Onboarding Sprint** (10 items per day, taking just 10–15 minutes a day):
- **Day 1**: Everyday Bottoms (10 pairs of jeans, trousers, skirts, or shorts)
- **Day 2**: Everyday Tops (10 T-shirts, blouses, button-downs, or polos)
- **Day 3**: Layers, Knitwear & Outerwear (10 sweaters, blazers, jackets, or coats)
- **Day 4**: Footwear (10 pairs of sneakers, boots, loafers, or heels)
- **Day 5**: Dresses, Sets & Signature Accessories (10 one-pieces, bags, belts, or scarves)

By Day 5, you will have **50 core items** digitized — unlocking thousands of outfit combinations!

### Q: Can I import my clothes from another wardrobe app?
**A:** Yes! If you previously used apps like **Whering**, **Indyx**, **Alta**, **Acloset**, or **Stylebook**, go to **Add Item → Migrate Wardrobe** to import your existing catalog in **1 click** with automatic background cleanup and attribute mapping.

## 3. Photographing, Uploading & Editing Clothes

### Q: What is the best way to photograph a garment for 100% accurate AI cutout?
**A:** Follow the **Flat-Lay Golden Rule**:
1. Lay the garment **flat on the floor** in a bright, naturally lit room on a **contrasting-color surface** (place dark clothes on a light floor/sheet, and light clothes on a dark background).
2. Center the garment straight in the frame with a **small, even margin** around all edges so sleeves and hems are never cropped.
3. Smooth out wrinkles, unbutton jackets, and **unfold sleeves** slightly away from the body so the AI can clearly see the cut and neckline.
4. Step back slightly so your body and phone **cast zero shadows** across the fabric, hold your hands steady, and take the shot.

### Q: What is the difference between regular background removal and "Reconstruct"?
**A:**
- **Standard Cutout (Automatic)**: Runs automatically on upload (1 On-Premises AI Credit on Free). It removes the background and extracts all metadata tags while preserving your original photo.
- **Studio Reconstruction (Optional — "Nano Banana")**: Triggered when you tap **Reconstruct** (1 Reconstruction Credit). It uses generative AI to remove deep wrinkles, fix harsh lighting, and render the garment as a crisp, studio-lit ghost-mannequin product shot.

### Q: Why should I review and edit an item right after uploading it?
**A:** While DressApp's AI is highly accurate, ambient indoor lighting can occasionally make navy blue look black, or a camera angle might hide whether trousers are high-waisted or mid-rise. Taking **10 seconds** to verify the **Category, Color, Season, Warmth (1–5), and Dress Code** guarantees that the AI Stylist, Weather Engine, and Suitcase Planner never make a mistake when styling that piece.

### Q: Can I group matching items or tag many items at once?
**A:** Yes!
- **Group Tagging**: In your **Closet**, tap **Select Multiple**, choose the items you want to update (for example, 8 office shirts), and apply tags like `Work`, `All-Season`, or `Ironed` to all of them at once.
- **Coordinated Sets**: Link a matching blazer and trousers (or a two-piece tracksuit) as a **Set** so you can style them together or separately.

## 4. Using the AI Stylist & Suitcase Planner

### Q: How do I write the best prompt for the AI Stylist?
**A:** The on-premises AI Stylist works best when you give it **four clear pieces of context**:
> **[Occasion / Event] + [Weather / Location] + [Dress Code / Vibe] + [Anchor Item or Constraint]**

**Great Prompt Examples:**
- *"Build a smart-casual outfit for a rainy autumn dinner in London using my beige trench coat."*
- *"Suggest a comfortable business-casual look for a full day of office meetings in air conditioning, with flat shoes."*
- *"Which 3 versatile tops am I missing to maximize outfit combinations with my existing skirts and jeans?"*

### Q: What is the difference between Stylist "Outfit Curation" and a "Wardrobe Audit"?
**A:** DressApp's Stylist automatically detects your intent:
- **Mode 1 — Outfit Curation**: When you ask *"What should I wear to..."*, the Stylist selects **only real garments currently inside your digital closet** and displays visual outfit cards you can save or wear today.
- **Mode 2 — Analytical Wardrobe Audit**: When you ask *"What is missing from my closet?"* or *"How balanced is my autumn capsule?"*, the Stylist analyzes your entire inventory and recommends specific high-value pieces that would bridge gaps between your existing clothes.

### Q: How does the Suitcase Packing Assistant work?
**A:** Open **Suitcase**, enter your **destination city, travel dates, and planned activities** (e.g., sightseeing, business conference, beach dinner). DressApp checks the destination's climate and curates a **mix-and-match travel capsule** from your closet so you pack fewer items while having a complete outfit for every day of your trip. *(On the Free plan, each garment curated into the suitcase costs 1 On-Premises AI Credit).*

## 5. Outfit Planner, Insights, Shopping Assistant & Marketplace

### Q: Can I build outfits manually and schedule them on a calendar?
**A:** Yes! In the **Outfit Planner**, you can drag and drop garments onto an interactive **layering canvas** (or view them on a 2D mannequin), adjust layering order, save the look to your **Lookbook**, and assign it to a specific date on your **Outfit Diary** calendar.

### Q: What can I learn from Wardrobe Insights?
**A:** The **Wardrobe Insights (Stats)** dashboard shows your total wardrobe valuation, **Cost-Per-Wear (CPW)** rankings, color palette distribution, seasonal balance, and carbon/water sustainability impact — helping you spot neglected items and shop more mindfully.

### Q: How does the Shopping Assistant Chrome Extension help when I shop online?
**A:** When browsing supported online fashion retailers, the **DressApp Shopping Assistant** compares the store's sizing chart against your saved **3D Biometric Body Profile** to recommend your best size and shows how the new piece would pair with items you already own.

### Q: Can I sell, swap, or donate clothes I no longer wear?
**A:** Yes! Through the **Circular Marketplace**, you can list unworn garments from your closet for **Sale**, **Swap**, or **Rent**, or connect with local charities to **Donate** them. You can even test how a marketplace item looks alongside your own wardrobe in the virtual **Sandbox Room** before buying.

## 6. Privacy, Security & Troubleshooting

### Q: Are my photos and body measurements private?
**A:** Absolutely. Your biometric measurements and personal wardrobe photos are strictly private to your account. When using DressApp's on-premises **Eyes** engine, your garment images are processed directly on DressApp's dedicated private infrastructure. You only share items publicly if you explicitly choose to publish a listing on the Marketplace.

### Q: Why did my stats page or stylist look empty when I first joined?
**A:** Insights and outfit curation require clothes in your closet! Once you upload your first few garments (or migrate an existing wardrobe), your **Wardrobe Insights** charts, **Smart Suggestions**, and **AI Stylist** recommendations populate immediately.

### Q: Where can I get more help or connect with a human stylist?
**A:**
- Tap the **Help (?)** button at the top of any screen in DressApp for quick 6-step visual guides and **"Learn More"** deep-dives in all 13 languages.
- Visit the **Certified Experts Registry** inside DressApp to book 1-on-1 virtual closet audits or personal styling sessions with vetted professional fashion stylists.
