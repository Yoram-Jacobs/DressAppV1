## Quick Overview (At a Glance)

### The Everyday Wardrobe Challenge
Most people wear only **20% of their wardrobe 80% of the time** because their closet is hard to keep track of, choosing an outfit every morning takes too much time, and adding clothes to traditional wardrobe apps is slow and manual.

### How DressApp Solves It
DressApp removes manual work and morning decision fatigue with smart visual recognition and personal styling:
- **Effortless Closet Digitization**: Take a photo of a single item, a full-body mirror outfit, or several clothes laid out together—or import from an online store link, receipt, product QR code, or another wardrobe app. DressApp automatically detects the photo type, removes backgrounds, separates multiple items, and fills in key details (Category, Color, Fabric, Cut, Season, Dress Code, and Warmth).
- **Cumulative Free Credit System**: Free users receive **10 daily On-Premises AI Credits** that accumulate up to **300 credits over 30 days**, plus **5 one-time onboarding bonus Studio Reconstruction Credits** when signing up. By photographing a few items a day over 5 days, you automatically have **50 credits** — enough to digitize a 50-item core wardrobe for free.
- **Personal Styling for Real Life**: Your AI Stylist combines the clothes in your closet, local weather forecasts, your calendar events, modesty preferences, and body measurements to suggest complete head-to-toe outfits or highlight missing wardrobe basics.

## 1. Getting Started & Profile Setup

### Q: What is the very first thing I should do after signing up?
**A:** Complete your **Profile** before adding clothes! Open the **Profile** tab and fill in four helpful settings:
1. **Gender Preference**: Helps set the right default categories and fit recommendations.
2. **Modesty Preference**: Tells your AI Stylist your preferred coverage level so every outfit suggestion feels comfortable and natural to you.
3. **Home Location**: Connects local weather forecasts so DressApp never suggests a heavy winter coat on a warm morning.
4. **Body Measurements**: Enter **4 simple measurements** (Height, Weight, Waist, and Chest/Bra size). DressApp automatically estimates **6 additional body measurements** to provide accurate size advice and flattering outfit proportions.

### Q: How long does it take to digitize my closet?
**A:** You don't need to photograph your whole closet in one day! We recommend the **5-Day Starter Plan** (10 items a day, taking just a few minutes per day):
- **Day 1**: Everyday Bottoms (10 pairs of jeans, trousers, skirts, or shorts)
- **Day 2**: Everyday Tops (10 T-shirts, blouses, button-downs, or polos)
- **Day 3**: Knitwear, Hoodies & Outerwear (10 sweaters, hoodies, blazers, jackets, or coats)
- **Day 4**: Footwear (10 pairs of sneakers, boots, loafers, or heels)
- **Day 5**: Dresses, Sets & Accessories (10 one-pieces, traditional attire, bags, belts, or scarves)

By Day 5, you will have **50 core items** in your digital closet — unlocking thousands of outfit combinations!

### Q: Can I import my clothes from another wardrobe app?
**A:** Yes! On **Manager** and **Professional** plans, the **Wardrobe Migration Agent** in **Profile → Import Wardrobe** lets you bulk-import 50+ items and outfits from Whering, Acloset, Stylebook, or Indyx with automatic item separation, background removal, and detail recognition. (Free accounts use our on-premises Eyes AI model with a 50-item closet cap, so bulk Wardrobe Migration requires a Manager or Professional plan.)

## 2. AI Credits, Subscriptions & Inviting Friends

### Q: How do Free AI Credits work?
**A:** Free users have two credit balances visible in **Profile**:
1. **On-Premises AI Credits (10 Free Daily Credits)**: Used for everyday scanning and styling powered by **DressApp's on-premises AI model**. You receive **10 free credits every day**, and unused credits **accumulate for up to 30 days (up to 300 credits)** before resetting.
2. **Studio Reconstruction Credits (5 One-Time Onboarding Bonus Credits)**: A one-time welcome bonus provided when you sign up to let you try out studio garment repairs and the AddItem re-analyzer (Nano Banana). These bonus credits do not renew each month. Free users can purchase non-expiring AI credit packs anytime on the Pricing page to top up, or upgrade to a Manager or Professional plan for 100 monthly Studio Reconstruction credits.

### Q: How many On-Premises AI Credits does each action use on the Free plan?
**A:** Each action with DressApp's on-premises AI model uses **1 credit per garment or request**:
- **Single-garment photo scan**: **1 credit**
- **Multi-item photo scan** (e.g., 5 garments detected in one full-outfit or group photo): **5 credits** (1 credit per garment)
- **AI Stylist chat request**: **1 credit** per message
- **Suitcase packing**: **1 credit per garment** added to your trip list
- **Browsing your closet, filtering, editing item details, and building looks on the Outfit Planner**: **0 credits (Always Free!)**

### Q: How can I get more closet space and extra credits for free?
**A:** Share your personal invite link in **Profile → Invite Friends**! Every friend who joins through your link gives you **+10 permanent free closet slots** (expanding your Free closet from 50 up to **150 items**) plus bonus credits.

### Q: What happens if I run out of Studio Reconstruction credits or want unlimited daily AI?
**A:** You have two flexible options on the **Pricing** page:
- **Add-On Credit Packs (Never Expire)**: Purchase **10 credits ($3)**, **50 credits ($12)**, or **100 credits ($20)**. Purchased credits never expire, are only used after your included credits are spent, and **holding 15 or more AI reconstruction credits unlocks the Cluster Garment Upload pipeline on the Free plan**! Free users with any reconstruction credit balance (> 0) can also use the AddItem re-analyzer.
- **Upgrade to Wardrobe Manager ($10/mo or $100/yr)**: Unlocks **unlimited closet space**, **unlimited daily styling and scans** powered by our **cutting-edge AI model**, **100 monthly Studio Reconstruction credits**, **Cluster Garment Uploading** (closets, hangers, shoe racks, and shirt piles), the **Wardrobe Migration Agent**, morning outfit notifications, **Trend Scout**, and the **Marketplace**.

### Q: What happens if I demote from Manager or Professional back to the Free plan?
**A:** Manager and Professional users who demote to the Free plan lose their extra closet slots, **leaving only the last 50 added items in their closet**. Older closet assets beyond the 50 most recently added items are permanently removed. Before accepting a planned demotion, DressApp displays a clear warning about losing older closet assets and asks for your confirmation.

## 3. Photographing, Adding & Editing Clothes

### Q: What is the best way to photograph a garment for a clean cutout?
**A:** Follow four simple steps:
1. Lay the garment **flat** in a bright room on a **contrasting surface** (place dark clothes on a light surface, and light clothes on a dark surface).
2. Center the garment in the frame with a **small, even margin** around all edges so sleeves and hems are fully visible.
3. Smooth out wrinkles and **spread the sleeves** slightly so the AI can clearly see the sleeve length and neckline.
4. Make sure neither your body nor your phone casts a shadow across the fabric, hold your phone steady, and take the photo.

### Q: What is the difference between automatic background removal, "Reconstruct", and Cluster Garment Uploading?
**A:**
- **Automatic Background Removal**: Runs automatically whenever you add a single garment or worn outfit photo (1 On-Premises AI Credit per garment on the Free plan). It removes the background and fills in item details while keeping your real photo.
- **Studio Reconstruction (Optional)**: Runs when you tap **Reconstruct** (1 Studio Reconstruction Credit). It uses our **Nano Banana** AI model to smooth out deep wrinkles, balance lighting, and generate a crisp studio-style product image.
- **Cluster Garment Uploading (Manager & Professional Plans, or Free Users with 15+ AI Reconstruction Credits)**: A revolutionary feature that identifies and reconstructs **garments hanging in your closet or on a hanger, a rack of shoes, a pile of shirts, and more**—not just garments spread on a bed! You can photograph your physical closet directly, use fewer photos, and digitize your wardrobe in no time.
  - **How Free Users Unlock Cluster Upload**: Because cluster uploading uses Nano Banana AI reconstruction (up to 15 garments per photo), Free users unlock the Cluster pipeline whenever they hold **15 or more AI reconstruction credits** (by purchasing an AI credit pack on the Pricing page), or by upgrading to a Manager or Professional plan. If a Free user with fewer than 15 AI reconstruction credits uploads a cluster photo, DressApp prompts them to **skip the image, purchase AI credits, or upgrade their plan**.
  - **5 Fallbacks of Cluster Uploading**:
    1. It is good for solid colors or very simple plaid or striped patterns, and cannot analyze hidden prints and fine details.
    2. The reconstructed image rarely reflects the current garment condition.
    3. It may skip some garments.
    4. It can hallucinate patterns and colors.
    5. It consumes reconstruction credits.

### Q: Why should I check an item's details right after adding it?
**A:** Warm indoor lighting can occasionally make navy blue look black, or an angle might hide a garment's exact fabric weight. Taking **10 seconds** to verify the **Category, Color, Season, Warmth (1–5), and Dress Code** ensures your AI Stylist and Suitcase Planner always use the item accurately.

### Q: Can I edit several items at once or link matching pieces into a set?
**A:** Yes!
- **Multi-Select Editing**: In your **Closet**, tap **Select Multiple**, pick the items you want to update, and apply tags or seasonal updates to all of them at once.
- **Matching Sets & Suits**: Link a matching jacket and trousers (or a coordinated two-piece set) as a **Set** so they can be styled together or separately.

## 4. Using the AI Stylist & Suitcase Packing Assistant

### Q: How do I get the best outfit suggestions from the AI Stylist?
**A:** Give the Stylist **four short details** in your message:
> **[Occasion / Event] + [Weather / Location] + [Dress Code / Style] + [Favorite Item or Color]**

**Examples:**
- *"Build a smart-casual outfit for a rainy autumn dinner using my beige trench coat."*
- *"Suggest a comfortable office look for a full day of meetings in air conditioning, with flat shoes."*
- *"Which 3 versatile tops am I missing to create more outfits with my current skirts and jeans?"*

### Q: What is the difference between asking for an outfit and asking for a closet review?
**A:** The Stylist automatically adapts to your question:
- **Outfit Ideas**: When you ask *"What should I wear to..."*, the Stylist selects **only clothes currently in your digital closet** and shows complete outfit cards you can wear today or save to your calendar.
- **Wardrobe Review**: When you ask *"What is missing from my closet?"*, the Stylist checks the balance of your wardrobe and suggests specific new basics that would pair well with the clothes you already own.

### Q: How does the Suitcase Packing Assistant work?
**A:** Open **Suitcase**, enter your **destination, travel dates, and trip purpose** (e.g., vacation, business trip, weekend getaway). DressApp checks the weather forecast at your destination and builds a **mix-and-match packing list** from your closet so you pack lighter while having outfits ready for every day.

## 5. Outfit Planner, Wardrobe Insights, Shopping Assistant & Marketplace

### Q: Can I put outfits together myself and schedule them on a calendar?
**A:** Yes! In the **Outfit Planner**, you can arrange items on an interactive canvas, adjust layers, preview the look, and schedule it on your **Outfit Calendar** for any upcoming day.

### Q: What can I see in Wardrobe Insights?
**A:** The **Wardrobe Insights** screen shows your total closet value, **Cost-Per-Wear** rankings, most-worn and unworn items, color distribution, and seasonal balance — helping you make the most of what you own.

### Q: How does the Shopping Assistant help when I shop online?
**A:** When browsing online clothing stores, the **Shopping Assistant** compares the store's size chart with your saved **Body Measurements** to suggest your best size and shows how the new item would match clothes already in your closet.

### Q: Can I sell, swap, or donate clothes I no longer wear?
**A:** Yes! In the **Marketplace**, you can list unworn items from your closet for **Sale**, **Swap**, or **Rent**, or mark them to **Donate**. You can also preview how a Marketplace item looks alongside your own clothes before getting it.

## 6. Privacy, Security & Getting Help

### Q: Are my photos and body measurements private?
**A:** Yes. Your body measurements and wardrobe photos are private to your account. When using **DressApp's on-premises AI model**, your photos are processed directly on DressApp's dedicated private infrastructure. Items are only visible to others if you explicitly choose to list them on the public Marketplace.

### Q: Why did my Insights or Stylist screen look empty when I first joined?
**A:** Wardrobe statistics and outfit suggestions need clothes in your closet first! As soon as you add your first few garments, your **Wardrobe Insights** charts and **AI Stylist** suggestions become active right away.

### Q: Where can I get more help or book a human stylist?
**A:**
- Tap the **Help (?)** button at the top of any screen for quick step-by-step guides and **"Learn More"** articles in all 13 languages.
- Visit the **Experts Directory** inside DressApp to connect with certified professional fashion stylists for personal styling sessions.
