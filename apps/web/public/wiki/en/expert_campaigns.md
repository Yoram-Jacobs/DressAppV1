# Managing Your Campaigns (Expert Guide)

Track, update, pause, or extend your business promotions.

## Overview
As a verified Fashion Expert, you can manage your promotional campaigns directly from your **My Campaigns** page. To keep things simple, each campaign displays its status clearly, along with settings for pausing, extending, or deleting.

## Prerequisites
- A verified Fashion Professional account.
- An existing campaign (in Draft, Active, or Paused status).

## Campaign Status Guide
Each campaign shows a color-coded status badge:
- **Draft** (Grey): Created but not yet submitted. You can edit all details.
- **Pending Approval** (Yellow): Paid and sent to the admin queue. You cannot edit it while it is being reviewed.
- **Active** (Green): Currently live and visible to users in your local feed and ticker.
- **Paused** (Orange): Temporarily hidden from users.
- **Rejected** (Red): Admin rejected the campaign (a reason will be displayed). You can edit and resubmit.
- **Expired** (Grey): The campaign's end date has passed.

## Step-by-Step Actions

### 1. How to Pause or Resume a Campaign
If you get too busy or run out of stock, you can hide your campaign:
1. Go to **My Campaigns** and click the **Settings** (gear) icon next to your active campaign.
2. Click **Pause Campaign**. It will immediately be hidden from the public feed.
3. When you are ready to show it again, click the gear icon and select **Resume Campaign**.
4. **No wasted money**: When you resume, the system automatically extends your campaign's end date by the exact number of days it was paused!

### 2. How to Extend a Campaign
If your promotion is doing well and you want it to run longer:
1. Click the **Settings** (gear) icon and choose **Extend Campaign**.
2. Pick your new end date.
3. Look at the fee breakdown ($1.00 USD for each additional day).
4. Click **Proceed to Payment** and complete the PayPal checkout.
5. Extensions are **instantly approved** — they do not go back to the admin review queue.

### 3. How to Delete a Campaign
If you want to permanently remove a campaign:
1. Click the **Settings** (gear) icon and click **Delete Campaign**.
2. Confirm the warning. 
3. **Note**: Active campaigns are deleted immediately. Unused days are non-refundable.

## Troubleshooting
- **Cannot see the settings gear**: The gear icon is only visible on campaigns that are active, paused, or drafts. It is not visible on pending campaigns or expired ones.
- **Resume button disabled**: Make sure you have not reached your limit of 3 active campaigns. If you already have 3 active/pending campaigns, you must delete or let one expire before resuming another.
