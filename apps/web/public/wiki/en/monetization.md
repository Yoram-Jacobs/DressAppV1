# Subscriptions, Credit Packs & Billing

Understand your account limits, upgrade your closet space, and manage your AI styling credits.

## Overview
DressApp offers a flexible hybrid monetization model designed to welcome casual closet organizers while providing powerful tools for fashion enthusiasts, creators, and certified stylists:
- **Free Tier Baseline**: Store up to 50 garments for free (expandable up to 150 items max via referral bonuses at +10 slots per signup). Powered out-of-the-box by the fine-tuned DressApp Eyes v1 model with zero API key setup.
- **Manager Plan ($10/month or $100/year)**: Unlimited wardrobe capacity, morning outfit push scheduler, calendar synchronization, circular marketplace selling, advanced stats, and Trend Scout ranking filters.
- **Professional Plan ($15/month or $150/year)**: Tailored for solo stylists and fashion creators, adding sponsored Ad Campaign creation, expert stylist directory listing, priority support, and 100 monthly styling credits.
- **Prepaid Credit Packs**: Top up with non-expiring credit packs ($3 for 10, $12 for 50, $20 for 100 credits) whenever high-volume styling or photo reconstruction is needed.

## Prerequisites
- A registered DressApp account.
- A PayPal account, credit/debit card, or the Israeli Bit app (for local ILS checkout) for plan upgrades or credit top-ups.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Explore the Free Baseline**: Start organizing immediately with 50 items. Outfits are powered out-of-the-box by DressApp Eyes v1 with zero API keys required.
2. **Expand for Free via Referrals**: Share your personal invite link from Profile settings. Each friend who joins unlocks +10 permanent closet slots (up to 150 items max).
3. **Upgrade to Manager ($10/mo or $100/yr)**: Go to Profile settings > System Preferences > Closet & Subscription Limits and tap Pricing Page to unlock unlimited garments, morning scheduler alerts, calendar sync, and Trend Scout filters.
4. **Upgrade to Professional ($15/mo or $150/yr)**: Select Professional to create targeted fashion advertising campaigns, list yourself in the expert stylist directory, and gain priority support.
5. **Purchase Prepaid Credits (Never Expire)**: If you need additional styling credits, choose a 10 ($3), 50 ($12), or 100 ($20) credit pack. The system consumes monthly subscription credits first, protecting your permanent non-expiring balance.

## Expected Results
Instant activation of plan benefits upon subscription. Closet limits immediately update to unlimited, and badges reflect your active tier on your Profile dashboard.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Closet Limit Reached" alert**: You have filled your available slots (50 baseline or up to 150 with referrals). Archive unworn items, invite friends for extra slots (+10 per invite up to 150 max), or upgrade to Manager/Professional for unlimited capacity.
- **Credit pack not reflected after payment**: Refresh your Profile page; transaction webhooks capture and credit your account within seconds.
- **Subscription management**: You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime directly from your PayPal account or Profile settings without losing your uploaded closet items.

## Limitations
- Referral capacity bonuses apply up to a maximum cap of 150 items on the Free Tier.
- Monthly subscription credits do not roll over to subsequent billing cycles; purchased prepaid credit packs never expire.
