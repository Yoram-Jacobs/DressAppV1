# What You Need to Get Started

DressApp works immediately after signing in, with no technical setup required. Here is everything you need for the best experience:

## 1. Device & Browser
- **Supported Devices**: Any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a modern web browser (such as Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox) and an internet connection.

## 2. Optional Device Permissions
- **Camera & Photo Library**: Needed to take pictures of your clothes or upload saved photos from your device.
- **Microphone**: Needed if you want to talk out loud with your AI Stylist (you can also type in the chat anytime).
- **Location**: Recommended so your AI Stylist can check local weather forecasts and show nearby Marketplace listings and local offers.

## 3. Built-In AI Models
- **Free Plan**: Includes **DressApp's on-premises AI model** right out of the box at no cost.
- **Manager & Professional Plans**: Unlock priority access to our faster **cutting-edge AI model**, unlimited closet storage, and daily fashion trend feeds.
