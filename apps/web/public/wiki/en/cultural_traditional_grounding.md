# Cultural & Traditional Attire Grounding Guide

## Overview
DressApp provides culturally grounded styling intelligence across 13 languages. Rather than relying on small-language-model probabilistic guesses—which frequently hallucinate or confuse distinct religious rites—DressApp enforces explicit, human-audited **Ground-Truth Axioms**. 

This document defines the canonical attire protocols, silhouette requirements, strict negative prohibitions, and localized linguistic terminologies across global traditions.

---

## 1. Jewish Traditions (יהדות)

### 1.1 Shiva & Mourning Etiquette (שבעה, אבלות וניחום אבלים)
- **Occasion Type**: Somber, condolence call, memorial, funeral.
- **Formality**: Respectful, solemn, understated.
- **Color Palette**: Solid black, charcoal, dark slate, deep navy, dark brown, or subdued muted earth tones. Avoid bright primaries, pastels, white, gold, or red.
- **Silhouettes & Cuts**:
  - **Men**: Clean tailored trousers or dark unadorned jeans; plain dark collared button-down, polo, or neat crewneck tee; dark subdued shoes or loafers.
  - **Women**: Modest midi/maxi dresses, long skirts, or tailored trousers; tops with sleeves covering shoulders and upper arms; conservative neckline.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO graphic t-shirts, loud logos, eagle/animal prints, slogan tees, or comedy elements.
  - NO shorts, distressed/ripped denim, gym/workout wear, or beach slides.
  - NO vibrant red, yellow, hot pink, gold, or neon colors.
  - NO flashy luxury jewelry or statement watches.
- **Language & Cultural Lexicon**:
  - *Approved Hebrew Terms*: `לביקור שבעה`, `לניחום אבלים`, `לשבעה`, `בשבעה`.
  - *Prohibited Machine-Translation Artifacts*: Never use literal translations like `להולך בישיבה שבעה` or `בישיבה שבעה`.

### 1.2 Orthodox Modesty (צניעות - Tzniut)
- **Occasion Type**: Daily, synagogue, community, sacred gatherings.
- **Formality**: Conservative, dignified.
- **Requirements**:
  - **Women**: Skirts and dresses extending below the knee even when seated; sleeves extending past the elbow; neckline covering the collarbone; non-transparent and non-clinging fabrics. For married Orthodox women, head covering (sheitel, tichel, or hat).
  - **Men**: Long trousers, collared shirts with sleeves, head covering (kippah / yarmulke).
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO trousers, jeans, or shorts for orthodox women.
  - NO sleeveless or cap-sleeve tops without an opaque layer (blazer/cardigan).
  - NO deep V-necks, low backs, sheer textiles, or high slit skirts.

### 1.3 Shabbat & Festive Holidays (שבת וחגים)
- **Occasion Type**: Joyous religious celebration, Friday evening dinner, holiday services.
- **Formality**: Elevated smart-casual to formal.
- **Color Palette**: Crisp white, cream, navy, sky blue, jewel tones, refined pastels.
- **Silhouettes**:
  - **Men**: Crisp white or light blue collared shirt, tailored dress trousers, blazer or sports jacket, polished dress shoes.
  - **Women**: Festive elegant midi/maxi dresses, tailored skirts with fine blouses, elegant accessories.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO workwear, athletic apparel, distressed clothing, or casual loungewear.

### 1.4 Synagogue Worship & Western Wall (בית כנסת ותפילה)
- **Occasion Type**: Prayer services (Shacharit, Mincha, Arvit), Torah reading, Western Wall (Kotel).
- **Formality**: Dignified, respectful (Kavod HaTzibbur).
- **Requirements**: Men wear long trousers or neat chinos, collared shirt or blazer, closed shoes, and mandatory head covering (Kippah). Women wear knee-covering skirts or dresses with covered shoulders and elbows.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO bare heads for men.
  - NO shorts, tank tops, crop tops, gym clothes, graphic prints, or beach flip-flops.

### 1.5 Brit Milah & Simchas (ברית מילה ושמחות)
- **Occasion Type**: Circumcision, baby naming (Simchat Bat), Bar/Bat Mitzvah, Pidyon HaBen.
- **Formality**: Joyful smart-formal / cocktail.
- **Color Palette**: Celebrating new life: soft pastels, sky blue, lavender, sage, cream, navy.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - Avoid somber mourning all-black funeral suits and black ties.
  - NO casual athletic apparel, distressed jeans, or gym sneakers.

### 1.6 Tisha B'Av & Yom Kippur Footwear Protocol (נעילת הסנדל)
- **Occasion Type**: Yom Kippur day of atonement, Fast of Tisha B'Av.
- **Requirements**: Worshippers wear non-leather footwear made exclusively of canvas, rubber, cotton, or synthetic materials. Yom Kippur attire is traditionally pure white; Tisha B'Av attire is somber mourning dress.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **STRICT PROHIBITION ON LEATHER FOOTWEAR**: Halakha strictly forbids real leather or suede shoes, boots, or sandals (Ne'ilat HaSandal).

## 2. Islamic Traditions (الإسلام)

### 2.1 Jumu'ah & Mosque Visitation (صلاة الجمعة وزيارة المسجد)
- **Occasion Type**: Friday congregational prayer, sacred mosque entry.
- **Formality**: Clean, modest, respectful.
- **Requirements**:
  - **Men**: Clean long trousers, thobe, or kurta; shoulders and chest fully covered; clean socks (shoes removed at entrance).
  - **Women**: Loose-fitting abaya, maxi dress, or long tunic over loose trousers; full coverage to wrists and ankles; hijab covering hair, ears, and neck.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO shorts above or at the knee for men (awrah requirement).
  - NO tight bodycon clothing, sheer fabrics, or low-cut necklines.
  - NO clothing depicting faces, human idols, or animal prints inside prayer spaces.

### 2.2 Hajj & Umrah Pilgrimage (الإحرام ومناسك الحج والعمرة)
- **Occasion Type**: Sacred pilgrimage in Makkah and Madinah.
- **Requirements**:
  - **Men**: Two seamless, unstitched white terrycloth/cotton cloths (Izar and Rida) secured with travel belt; simple sandals leaving ankles and foot bridge uncovered.
  - **Women**: Loose, modest clothing (neutral abaya) covering the entire body except face and hands.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - Men: STRICTLY FORBID stitched garments, trousers, shirts, jackets, underwear, socks, or head coverings.
  - Women: STRICTLY FORBID the Niqab (face veil) and gloves during Ihram (face and hands must remain uncovered).

### 2.3 Eid al-Fitr & Eid al-Adha Festive Splendor (عيد الفطر وعيد الأضحى)
- **Occasion Type**: Eid morning prayer and festive family feasts.
- **Formality**: Elevated celebratory elegance following the Sunnah of wearing one's finest, cleanest clothes.
- **Silhouettes**: Embroidered Kaftans, Abayas, pristine Thobes/Kanduras, tailored suits, or Kurtas.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO worn-out, stained, distressed casualwear, or faded graphic tees.

### 2.4 Regional Modest Attire Protocols
- **Arabian Gulf Formal Protocol (البشت والكندورة)**: Tailored floor-length white or wool Thobe with Ghutra/Shemagh and Agal, draped with an embroidered gold *Zari* **Bisht** cloak for formal weddings and diplomacy. Never style a Bisht with jeans or casual t-shirts.
- **North African Djellaba & Babouche (الجلابة والبلغة)**: Fine-wool hooded Djellaba over a Gandora with handcrafted leather Belgha/Babouche slippers.
- **Southeast Asian Batik & Baju Melayu (Batik & Baju Melayu)**: Formal long-sleeved silk Batik shirts substitute for Western business suits in Indonesia/Malaysia. Ceremonial Baju Melayu includes the Songket Kain Samping waist-wrap and black velvet Songkok cap.

## 3. Hindu Traditions (सनातन धर्म)

### 3.1 Funerals & Mourning (Antyeshti - अंतिम संस्कार)
- **Occasion Type**: Hindu cremation, funeral procession, bereavement.
- **Formality**: Solemn, pure, austere.
- **Color Palette**: **Solid, plain, unadorned white only**.
- **Silhouettes**:
  - **Men**: Plain white kurta-pyjama or white cotton shirt with light neutral pants.
  - **Women**: Simple white cotton saree or white salwar kameez without embellishment.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION ON BLACK**: Black is considered inauspicious and strictly forbidden at Hindu funerals.
  - NO vibrant colors (red, gold, orange, pink).
  - NO leather shoes or leather belts inside sacred cremation grounds or temple rituals.

### 3.2 Weddings & Festive Celebrations (Vivaha & Diwali - विवाह उत्सव)
- **Occasion Type**: Hindu wedding ceremonies (Sangeet, Baraat, Pheras), Diwali, auspicious festivals.
- **Formality**: Opulent, celebratory, high formal.
- **Color Palette**: Auspicious reds, deep maroons, gold, saffron, royal emerald, rani pink.
- **Silhouettes**: Sherwani, Bandhgala, Kurta with Nehru jacket for men; Lehenga, Kanjeevaram/Banarasi saree, Anarkali for women.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **PROHIBITION ON SOLID BLACK**: Black is strictly avoided at Hindu wedding ceremonies.
  - **PROHIBITION ON PLAIN WHITE**: Solid plain white is associated with widowhood/mourning and is strictly avoided by wedding guests.

### 3.3 Hindu Temple Darshan & Non-Leather Sanctum Protocol (मंदिर दर्शन)
- **Occasion Type**: Temple Darshan, Puja, and sacred circumambulation.
- **Requirements**: Modest traditional attire (Kurta-pyjama, Dhoti, Saree, Salwar Kameez with Dupatta). Shoulders and knees fully covered. Footwear removed outside at Charan Paduka.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **ABSOLUTE BAN ON REAL LEATHER**: Leather shoes, belts, wallets, watch straps, or bags are strictly forbidden inside temple sanctums (Ahimsa violation).
  - NO shorts, mini skirts, or bare shoulders.

### 3.4 South Indian Kerala Temple Attire (Mundu & Kasavu)
- **Occasion Type**: Orthodox Kerala temple inner sanctums (Guruvayur, Padmanabhaswamy).
- **Requirements**: Men must enter inner sanctums bare-chested wearing a pristine off-white cotton **Mundu** with gold *Kasavu* border and folded Melmundu stole. Women wear cream-and-gold Kasavu sarees or Set Mundu.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - Strictly forbid stitched shirts, t-shirts, Western trousers, or jeans for men in orthodox sanctums.

## 4. Buddhist Sacred Traditions (พุทธศาสนา / 佛教)

### 4.1 Temple Visitation & Monastic Color Taboo (วัดวาอาราม)
- **Occasion Type**: Visiting wats, stupas, pagodas, and monasteries (Thailand, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Japan, Tibet).
- **Requirements**: Modest, loose, calm clothing. Shoulders, upper arms, and knees must be fully covered (ankle-length bottoms preferred). Shoes and hats removed before entering shrine halls.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **STRICT PROHIBITION ON MONASTIC COLORS**: Lay visitors must NEVER wear solid saffron, golden-orange, ochre, or Tibetan maroon robes (reserved exclusively for ordained Buddhist monks / Bhikkhus).
  - NO sleeveless tops, tank tops, short shorts, mini skirts, or sheer fabrics.
  - NO irreverent Buddha prints on trousers, shoes, or casual shirts.

### 4.2 Lay Meditation & Precept Observance (ชุดขาวปฏิบัติธรรม / Chut Khao)
- **Occasion Type**: Vipassana silent meditation retreats, Uposatha observance days, Vesak.
- **Requirements**: **Entirely pure solid white attire** (Chut Khao). White signifies simplicity, mental purity, renunciation of vanity, and equality.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO bright, multicolored, saturated garments.
  - NO jewelry, makeup, loud patterns, or tight activewear leggings.

## 5. Sikh Sacred Gurdwara Protocol (ਗੁਰਦੁਆਰਾ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਮਰਯਾਦਾ)

- **Occasion Type**: Visiting a Sikh Gurdwara, Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib), Darbar Sahib.
- **Formality**: Humility, equality, and reverence.
- **Requirements**:
  - **MANDATORY HEAD COVERING**: Every person must cover their head entirely with a **Rumāl** (cloth scarf), **Patka**, **Dastar** (turban), or **Chunni**.
  - Bare feet: Shoes and socks removed at Joda Ghar; feet washed in foot pool (Charanamrit).
  - Modest loose clothing (Kurta Pyjama, Salwar Kameez, or modest pants/blouses) suitable for sitting cross-legged.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **STRICT PROHIBITION ON CAPS & HATS**: Baseball caps, fedoras, beanies, or sunhats are strictly barred as head coverings.
  - NO bare heads.
  - NO shorts, mini skirts, sleeveless tops, or bare shoulders.
  - ABSOLUTELY NO TOBACCO, SMOKING MATERIALS, OR ALCOHOL ON PERSON.

## 6. East Asian Ceremonial & Cultural Nuances (东亚礼仪 / 日本の伝統 / 한국의 전통)

### 6.1 Funerals & Memorials (葬礼 / お葬式 / 장례식)
- **Occasion Type**: Chinese, Japanese, Korean funerals and ancestor memorials.
- **Requirements**: Tailored solid black suit, crisp white dress shirt, plain matte black necktie; dark modest dress or kimono for women.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION ON RED OR GOLD**: In East Asian culture, red and gold symbolize joy and celebration; wearing them to a funeral is an extreme insult.

### 6.2 Wedding Guest Etiquette (婚礼 / 結婚式 / 결혼식)
- **Color Palette**: Refined festive colors (pastels, navy, grey, blush, jewel tones).
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **NO SOLID RED FOR GUESTS**: At Chinese weddings, red is reserved exclusively for the bride.
  - **NO SOLID WHITE FOR GUESTS**: Solid white is reserved for the bridal gown.

### 6.3 Japanese Kimono Left-Over-Right Rule (着物の左前)
- **Traditional Garments**: Kimono, Yukata, Haori, Jinbei.
- **Requirements**: Wearers must ALWAYS wrap the **left collar over the right collar** (*Hidari-mae*). Worn with white split-toe Tabi socks and Zōri sandals.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **STRICT PROHIBITION ON RIGHT-OVER-LEFT**: Right-over-left collar wrapping (*Migi-mae*) is exclusively reserved for dressing corpses for cremation funerals; wearing it on a living person is an ominous death taboo.

### 6.4 Korean Hanbok Wedding Palette (혼주 한복)
- **Occasion Type**: Korean traditional weddings (*Paebaek*).
- **Confucian Maternal Protocol**:
  - **Mother of the Bride**: Strictly wears **warm tones** (pink, coral, peach, soft red).
  - **Mother of the Groom**: Strictly wears **cool tones** (sky blue, indigo, navy, jade green, cyan).
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - Mothers must never invert maternal colors. Guests must avoid bridal white.

### 6.5 Chinese Green Hat Taboo (戴绿帽子 - Dài Lǜ Màozi)
- **Cultural Taboo**: In Chinese society, "wearing a green hat" (*dài lǜ màozi*) signifies cuckoldry (that a man's wife has committed adultery).
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **STRICTLY PROHIBIT GREEN HEADWEAR FOR MEN**: Never style men with green caps, green beanies, green fedoras, or green hats.

## 7. African Ceremonial Traditions (West Africa, Southern Africa)

### 7.1 Yoruba Ceremonial Agbada, Gele & Aso Ebi (Àgbàdá àti Gèlè)
- **Occasion Type**: Nigerian weddings, chieftaincy celebrations, high-society galas.
- **Requirements**: Men wear the majestic 3-piece **Agbada** (inner Buba, Sokoto trousers, flowing Agbada robe) crowned by an embroidered **Fila** cap tilted to one side. Women wear Buba, Iro wrap skirt, sculpted **Gele** head-tie, and Ipele shoulder sash conforming to host Aso Ebi colors.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - Never wear an Agbada without the Fila cap (considered incomplete). Do not let Agbada sleeves drag on the floor.

### 7.2 Igbo Chieftaincy Isiagu & Coral Regalia (Ịsị Àgụ)
- **Occasion Type**: Igbo traditional weddings (*Igba Nkwu*), title taking.
- **Requirements**: Men wear the **Isiagu** tunic (featuring lion/leopard head embroidery) paired with plain black trousers, Okpu Agu knit cap, and cylindrical royal coral neck beads (*Ijele*).
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - Forbid pairing Isiagu with patterned or clashing bottoms (must be solid black/dark slacks to anchor the statement print).

### 7.3 Ghanaian Akan/Ashanti Kente & Funeral Kobene (Kente & Kobene)
- **Occasion Type**: Celebrations, weddings, durbars vs Akan funerals.
- **Celebration Requirements**: Multicolored handwoven royal Kente cloth draped toga-style over the **left shoulder** for men (leaving right arm free); 3-piece Kaba and Slit for women.
- **Funeral Protocol**: Multicolored Kente is strictly forbidden at funerals. Mourners wear **Kobene** (deep vermilion red) or **Kuntunkuni** (dark brown/black indigo cloth).
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - STRICTLY FORBID JOYOUS GOLD/MULTICOLORED KENTE AT FUNERALS.
  - Never drape male Kente toga over the right shoulder.

### 7.4 Zulu Heritage Etiquette (Isicholo & Shweshwe)
- **Occasion Type**: Traditional Zulu weddings (*Umabo*), heritage festivals.
- **Requirements**: Married Zulu women wear the flared conical **Isicholo** hat with beaded capes. Celebratory dresses crafted from indigo-printed **Shweshwe** cotton are standard formal attire across southern Africa.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - Unmarried women do not wear the Isicholo. Prohibit casual gym wear at traditional ceremonies.

## 8. Latin American Traditions & Catholic Milestones

### 8.1 Formal Guayabera Protocol (Guayabera de Gala)
- **Geographic Region**: Mexico (Yucatán), Cuba, Dominican Republic, Colombia (Cartagena), Panama, Puerto Rico.
- **Formality**: Recognized as tropical Black Tie / formal suit substitute for diplomatic functions and formal weddings.
- **Requirements**: 100% crisp fine linen, 4 front pockets, vertical alforza pleats front and back, side button vents, straight finished hem designed to be **worn strictly UNTUCKED**, paired with tailored dress trousers and leather loafers.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **NEVER TUCK IN A GUAYABERA**: Tucking a Guayabera destroys its vents and pleats and is an amateur sartorial blunder.
  - Forbid short-sleeved casual Guayaberas at formal evening weddings. Forbid sneakers, flip-flops, or jeans.

### 8.2 Quinceañera & Catholic Milestone Etiquette (Quince Años)
- **Occasion Type**: Catholic Mass of Thanksgiving followed by evening gala.
- **Requirements**: Semi-formal to formal suits for men; elegant formal cocktail or midi dresses with covered shoulders during church liturgy for women.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - Female guests must NEVER wear a white or ivory ball gown or tiara (reserved strictly for the Quinceañera celebrant).
  - Maintain church modesty during the preliminary Mass (no plunging necklines or bare shoulders).

## 9. Christian Church & Western Formal Standards

### 9.1 Christian Church, Cathedral & Holy Sanctuary Etiquette (כנסייה, מיסה ואתרים קדושים)
- **Occasion Type**: Sunday Mass, Cathedral visitation, pilgrimage (Bethlehem Church of the Nativity, Holy Sepulchre, Vatican).
- **Requirements**: Shoulders, chest, and knees fully covered. Men wear smart collared shirts, tailored trousers or dark chinos, and closed dress shoes/loafers. Women wear modest dresses covering knees or tailored slacks with covered shoulders.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO graphic t-shirts, loud slogan prints, eagle or animal prints, cartoon graphics.
  - NO ripped/distressed denim, tank tops, crop tops, shorts, beachwear, flip-flops, or hats/caps inside the sanctuary.

### 9.2 Vatican & Papal Audience Protocol (Privilège du Blanc)
- **Occasion Type**: Private audience with the Pope at the Apostolic Palace.
- **Requirements**: Men wear dark conservative suit (charcoal or navy) with subdued tie and black oxford shoes. Women wear long-sleeved black dress or skirt suit falling well below the knee, high neckline, and black lace mantilla.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - **NO WHITE DRESSES FOR WOMEN**: The *Privilège du blanc* is reserved exclusively for reigning Catholic queens and royal princesses.
  - NO sleeveless tops, bare shoulders, hemlines above the knee, open-toed sandals, athletic shoes, or casual caps.

### 9.3 Eastern Orthodox & Coptic Sanctuary Etiquette
- **Occasion Type**: Divine Liturgy, Orthodox monasteries (Mount Athos, Saint Catherine's Sinai).
- **Requirements**: Long trousers and collared shirts for men (hats removed). Modest below-the-knee skirts or dresses for women with covered shoulders and hair scarf/foulard. Shoes removed in Coptic Holy of Holies.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - Women are strictly forbidden from wearing trousers/pants in traditional Orthodox monasteries (skirts only).
  - NO shorts, sleeveless tops, or casual caps.

### 9.4 Western Black Tie & Gala Events
- **Occasion Type**: Charity galas, opera openings, formal evening receptions.
- **Requirements**: Men wear black or midnight blue tuxedo with satin lapels, matching trousers with side stripe, white pique/pleated tuxedo shirt, black silk bow tie, and patent leather shoes. Women wear floor-length evening gowns.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - NO daytime business suits, long neckties, button-down collars, chinos, sneakers, or casual loafers.

### 9.5 Western White Tie Protocol (Cravate Blanche)
- **Occasion Type**: State banquets, royal coronations, Nobel ceremonies.
- **Requirements**: Black wool tailcoat cut short in front with dual swallowtails, matching trousers with double side braid, white piqué stiff wing-collar shirt, white piqué low-cut waistcoat, hand-tied white piqué bow tie, and black patent court pumps/oxfords. Women wear formal floor-length ball gowns with opera gloves.
- **Strict Negative Prohibitions**:
  - Strictly forbid standard tuxedos, black bow ties with tails (reserved for waitstaff), colored waistcoats, daytime neckties, or short cocktail dresses.
