# Trend Scout & Personal Inspiration

Discover daily curated fashion feeds tailored to your style preferences.

## Overview
Trend Scout aggregates street, runway, sustainability, and influencer feeds, sorting them dynamically based on your occupation and style rules.

## Prerequisites
- Local region location services enabled.
- User occupation field filled in your Profile settings.

## Step-by-Step
1. **Open Scout**: Navigate to the Trends screen.
2. **Explore Feeds**: Browse cards across runway collections and eco-friendly style boards.
3. **Save Looks**: Bookmark ideas or find gaps in your closet matching current trends.
4. **Auto-translate**: Feeds automatically translate to your active language.

## Expected Results
A daily updated feed of relevant style cards matching your regional demographic profile.

## Troubleshooting
- **Feed not updating**: The Trend Scout background scheduler updates content once every 24 hours.
- **Irrelevant cards**: Ensure your profile occupation and style rule preferences are accurate to improve ranking.

## Limitations
- Custom demographic rankings are calculated using server-side preferences.
