# Trend Scout & Personal Inspiration

Discover daily global fashion trends, local style radar, and recreate trending looks instantly using clothes from your own closet.

---

## 1. Overview
Trend Scout is your daily fashion intelligence radar inside DressApp. Every morning, it gathers breaking fashion reports, runway reviews, sustainable textile innovations, and street style movements from reputable fashion publications worldwide.

Organized into **7 curated channels**, Trend Scout personalizes style stories to your demographic profile and location. With the signature 1-tap **"Style with My Closet"** feature, our AI Stylist scans the aesthetic traits of any trend (color palette, silhouette, textures) and highlights matching garments already hanging in your digitized wardrobe—letting you wear high-fashion trends today without buying anything new!

---

## 2. Prerequisites
To get the most out of Trend Scout, ensure you have:
- **Custom AI Supplier API Key (BYOK)**: Trend Scout requires a personal AI supplier API key (such as Google Gemini, obtainable for free via [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/)) configured under **Profile > AI Configuration** to power live article crawling and synthesis.
- **An Active Plan**: Trend Scout is available on the **Manager** ($10/mo) and **Professional** ($15/mo) plans. Free accounts can view a preview and upgrade at any time.
- **Digitized Wardrobe Items**: At least 5 to 10 garments in your digital closet so the AI Stylist can provide accurate "Style with My Closet" outfit proposals.
- **Location Access**: Allows Trend Scout to anchor local news, regional boutique highlights, and domestic fashion events to your city and country.
- **Demographics in Settings**: Specifying your gender, lifestyle, and aesthetic preferences ensures your feed prioritizes looks tailored to your personal taste.

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Open Trend Scout
1. From the main navigation, click or tap **Trend Scout** (look for the trending icon 📈 in your menu or Stylist sidebar).
2. The page loads with your personalized Daily Edit feed anchored to your home country.

### Step 2: Browse the 7 Curated Channels
Filter stories by selecting the category tabs along the top of your feed:
- 📍 **Local News**: Regional fashion events, domestic designer debuts, and local boutique news in your area.
- 👑 **Runway**: High-fashion reviews, seasonal Haute Couture shows, and emerging designer trends.
- 👟 **Street Style**: Everyday urban wear, sneaker culture, and contemporary casual ensembles.
- 🌿 **Sustainability**: Eco-friendly fashion, circular textiles, zero-waste initiatives, and ethical garment care.
- ✨ **Influencers & Icons**: Viral social styling movements, red carpet highlights, and creator-driven aesthetics.
- ♻️ **Vintage / Archival**: Thrift culture, pre-loved curation, retro denim, and timeless archival fashion.
- 🔧 **Care & Repairs**: Practical guides for garment longevity, delicate fabric care, mending, and sneaker restoration.

### Step 3: Switch Gender Targeting
- Toggle between **Women's Fashion** and **Men's Fashion** at any time using the pill selector in the header.
- The feed dynamically filters articles and recalculates recommendations based on the selected gender.

### Step 4: 1-Tap "Style with My Closet"
1. When you spot a trend you love, look at the trend card.
2. Click the **Style with My Closet** button.
3. The AI Stylist instantly opens with the trend's aesthetic parameters and displays pieces from your closet that replicate the look.
4. Preview the resulting outfit on your 2D character avatar and save it to your Wardrobe Diary!

### Step 5: Customize Personalization & Social Feeds (Settings ⚙️)
1. Click the **Settings (gear icon ⚙️)** in the Trend Scout header.
2. **Style Aesthetics**: Tap from 10 curated style tags (such as *Quiet Luxury*, *Vintage*, *Minimalist*, *Streetwear*, *Old Money*, *Boho & Casual*, *Cyberpunk*, *Y2K*, *Classic Business*, or *Athleisure*), or type your own custom aesthetic.
3. **Connected Platforms**: Link your social media handles (Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, Facebook, Threads, or X) so DressApp aligns with your broader fashion interests.
4. **Closet Profile**: Review your closet’s color and silhouette profile automatically computed by DressApp.
5. Tap **Save & Refresh Feed** to immediately retarget your daily trend cards.

### Step 6: Read Full Authentic Articles & Real-Time Refresh
- Tap **"Read at [Publication]"** on any card to open the original article directly on publications like Vogue, GQ, Elle, or Hypebeast.
- Need the latest breaking stories right now? Click the **Refresh (🔄)** button in the header to run an on-demand live radar sweep.

## 5. Troubleshooting

### My feed says "Trend Scout requires a custom API key (403)"
- **Cause**: You have not yet added a personal Google Gemini API key to your account.
- **Solution**: Open **Profile > AI Configuration**, switch to Custom Key Mode, and enter your free key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/).

### My feed says "Trend Scout is Premium"
- **Cause**: Your account is currently on the Free tier.
- **Solution**: Tap **Upgrade Plan** to subscribe to the Manager plan ($10/mo) or Professional plan ($15/mo).

### The cards are not showing my local country's news
- **Cause**: Location permissions may be turned off or your profile address is unconfigured.
- **Solution**: Check your browser or mobile app location permissions, or set your home country under **Profile > Settings > Location Services**.

### I see articles that don't match my preferred style or gender
- **Cause**: The gender toggle may be set to the opposite mode, or your aesthetic tags are empty.
- **Solution**: Switch the **Women's / Men's** toggle in the header, or open **Settings ⚙️** and select your preferred style aesthetics.

### Cards aren't refreshing or show older dates
- **Cause**: Network connectivity issues or cached feed data.
- **Solution**: Tap the **Refresh (🔄)** button in the header (or swipe down to pull-to-refresh on mobile) to fetch live data.

## 6. Limitations
- **Custom API Key Required**: Trend Scout is available exclusively for users with their own custom AI supplier API key (Google Gemini).
- **Editorial Focus**: Trend Scout is designed for pure fashion inspiration and education. It deliberately filters out aggressive shopping advertisements, affiliate pop-ups, and commercial sales carts.
- **Closet Matching**: The AI can only style trends using items you have digitized. The more clothes you scan into your closet, the more creative your trend outfit proposals will be!
- **Offline Mode**: While previously viewed cards and guides are cached, live crawling and reading external source articles require an active internet connection.
