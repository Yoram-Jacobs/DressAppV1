## 1. Executive Summary & Architectural Purpose

When an AI Stylist or Suitcase Packing Assistant recommends outfits for international travel, domestic neighborhoods, or religious precincts, **probabilistic fashion aesthetics are insufficient—and potentially dangerous**. Recommending a camouflage backpack for a trip to Barbados, an Adidas three-stripe tracksuit in a contested Rio de Janeiro or Salvador favela, shorts at the Grand Palace in Bangkok, a leather belt inside a Jain temple in Ranakpur, or a full-face niqab in Switzerland or France can expose the user to:
1. **Criminal Prosecution, Arrest, Confiscation, or Fines** (e.g., Defence Acts, Public Decency Regulations, Secular Face-Covering Bans, Lèse-Majesté statutes).
2. **Morality Police Detention, Corporal Punishment, or Deportation** (e.g., Iran's *Gasht-e Ershad* / *Noor Plan*, Afghanistan's *Muhtasibin*, Aceh's *Wilayatul Hisbah*).
3. **Lethal Paramilitary, Cartel, or Gang Retaliation** (e.g., Brazilian drug-faction brand/color codes, Central American *Maras* numeric/shoe codes, Jamaican garrison colors).
4. **Immediate Denial of Entry or Expulsion from Sacred Precincts** (e.g., Vatican City, Mount Athos, Haramain, Kerala Hindu temples, Shwedagon Pagoda).

This document establishes the **human-audited, primary-source-verified Ground-Truth Catalog** across all seven restriction domains (`A` through `G`), linking every rule to its governing statute, ministerial decree, consular travel advisory, or ecclesiastical authority.

## Domain A: State-Mandated Religious & Statutory Modesty Regimes

### A.1 Islamic Republic of Iran
- **Primary Legal Authorities**:
  - **Islamic Penal Code of Iran (Book 5, *Ta'zirat* / Discretionary Punishments), Article 638 & Binding Note**: Criminalizes any act deemed *haram* (sinful) in public (10 days to 2 months imprisonment or up to 74 lashes). The **Note to Article 638** explicitly states: *"Women who appear in public places and roads without wearing an Islamic hijab shall be sentenced to ten days to two months' imprisonment or a fine of fifty thousand to five hundred thousand Rials."*
  - **2024 Law on Supporting the Family Through Promoting the Culture of Chastity and Hijab (74 Articles)** & **Law Enforcement Command (FARAJA) "Noor Plan" (*Tarh-e Noor*, launched April 2024)**: Institutionalizes AI-assisted CCTV/traffic surveillance, automated SMS citations, vehicle impoundment, bank-account deductions, travel bans, and escalated multi-tier monetary fines for "nudity" or "improper hijab" (*bad-hijabi*).
- **Mandatory Attire & Gender-Specific Rules**:
  - **Women (All Citizens, Residents & Foreign Tourists from Age 9 Lunar / ~8.7 Solar Years)**:
    - **Headwear**: Mandatory headscarf (*rusari* or *shawl*) covering hair, neck, and décolletage in all public spaces, streets, hotels, domestic/international flights landing in Iran, and public transport.
    - **Upper Body**: Loose-fitting tunic, overcoat, or *manteau* (*roopoosh*) covering arms at least to the wrists/forearms and extending down to at least mid-thigh or knee to conceal body contours (hips and bust).
    - **Lower Body**: Opaque long trousers, jeans (non-ripped), or ankle-length skirts. If wearing shorter skirts/dresses, opaque thick stockings/leggings are mandatory.
    - **Holy Shrines (Mashhad Imam Reza, Qom Fatima Masumeh, Shiraz Shah Cheragh)**: Standard street *manteau* + *rusari* is insufficient; women must wear a full-length **Chador** (loaned at shrine entrances if not owned).
  - **Men**:
    - **Lower Body**: Long trousers or jeans are required in all public streets and buildings. **Shorts above the knee are prohibited in public spaces** (except on enclosed sports fields or designated Caspian/Kish men-only beaches).
    - **Upper Body & Accessories**: Sleeveless tank tops (*zirپوش*) in public streets and Western neckties (*kravat*, viewed by state institutions as symbols of Western cultural influence) in government ministries, state broadcasters, and courts are restricted.
- **Penalties & Risks**: Morality police (*Gasht-e Ershad*) detention, automatic vehicle seizure if a female passenger removes her headscarf, business closure, fines escalating under the 2024 schedule from IRR 30 million to IRR 1.65 billion (~USD $50–$2,800+), exit bans, and prosecution up to 10 years imprisonment if accused of collaborating with foreign media/campaigns against mandatory hijab.

### A.2 Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan
- **Primary Legal Authorities**:
  - **Law on the Propagation of Virtue and the Prevention of Vice (*Qanun-e Amr bil-Ma'ruf wa Nahi 'an al-Munkar*)**, ratified by Supreme Leader Hibatullah Akhundzada and published in the Official Gazette (No. 1452) on **August 21, 2024** (4 Chapters, 35 Articles), enforced by the Ministry for the Propagation of Virtue and Prevention of Vice (*Muhtasibin*).
- **Mandatory Attire & Gender-Specific Rules**:
  - **Women (Article 13)**:
    - **Full-Body & Face Covering**: Article 13(1)–(2) mandates that a woman's entire body is *awrah* and must be veiled in public at all times, including a mandatory **face covering** (traditional blue pleated *chadori* / *burqa*, or full black *abaya* with *niqab* leaving only the eyes or a mesh screen visible) to prevent *fitnah* (temptation).
    - **Prohibited Garments**: Article 13(3) strictly prohibits clothing that is **thin/sheer, tight-fitting, or short**.
    - **Non-Muslim & Mahram Rules**: Article 13(4) mandates that Muslim women must cover themselves in front of non-Muslim women as well as all non-*mahram* men. Article 13(3) also classifies a woman's public voice (singing, reciting, speaking loudly) as *awrah*.
    - **Foreign Female Visitors / Humanitarian Workers**: Required to wear a loose headscarf covering all hair, long loose tunic/abaya over ankle-length trousers, and increasingly pressured at checkpoints to cover the face or travel with a *mahram* template/chaperone permit.
  - **Men (Article 14 & Civil Service Decrees)**:
    - **Male *Awrah* (Article 14(1))**: Strictly defined from the navel to and including the knee. Men are banned from wearing shorts, athletic wear exposing the knees, or tight trousers in public or during sports.
    - **Mandatory Beard & Haircut (Article 14(2))**: Shaving or trimming the beard shorter than one fist-length (*qabzah*) is a penal offense. Western-style haircuts (e.g., fades, undercuts) are banned; barbers violating this face shop closure and detention.
    - **Ban on Western Ties & Suits**: Article 14 and civil service directives prohibit neckties (deemed symbols of the Christian cross by the Ministry) and require male civil servants to wear traditional *Perahan Tunban* (loose tunic and trousers) with a turban (*lungi*) or skullcap (*kufi*).
- **Penalties & Risks (Article 24)**: Graduated enforcement by *Muhtasibin*: verbal admonition, intimidation, fine, property confiscation, and arbitrary detention in public jail for 1 to 3 days before referral to Taliban courts for corporal punishment (flogging) or imprisonment.

### A.3 Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
- **Primary Legal Authorities**:
  - **Public Decency Charter / Regulation (*La'ihat al-Dhawq al-'Amm*)**, Cabinet Decision No. 444 (April 2019), implemented by the Ministry of Interior in **September 2019** (19 scheduled violations).
  - **Royal Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Directives on the Haramain Boundaries (Mecca & Medina)**.
- **Mandatory Attire & Gender-Specific Rules**:
  - **General Public Spaces (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, AlUla)**:
    - While the mandatory black *abaya* and headscarf were lifted for foreign female visitors in September 2019 (and relaxed in practice for citizens in major cities), **both men and women must cover shoulders and knees in public** (Violation #19).
    - **Prohibited Items**: Tight-fitting, sheer/translucent clothing, crop tops, mini-skirts, and shorts above the knee for men or women in public streets, malls, and restaurants.
    - **Graphic & Slogan Ban**: Violation #10 & #19 strictly ban any garment, hat, or bag bearing profane language, political slogans, racist/sectarian imagery, drug/alcohol references, or sexually suggestive graphics.
    - **Ultra-Luxury Red Sea Private Resorts (e.g., Sindalah, Ummahat Islands, Sheybarah)**: Exempted by Red Sea Global special economic zone rules from mainland street dress codes (bikinis and shorts permitted on private resort beaches), though mainland Public Decency rules apply immediately upon transiting public airports.
  - **Sacred Precincts — Mecca (*Makkah al-Mukarramah*) & Medina (*Al-Madinah al-Munawwarah*)**:
    - **Non-Muslim Entry Ban**: Non-Muslims are strictly prohibited by law from entering the **Haram boundary of Mecca** (marked by highway checkpoints) and the sacred interior of **Al-Masjid an-Nabawi (Prophet's Mosque)** in Medina.
    - **Ihram Rules for Hajj & Umrah**: Male pilgrims entering the *Miqat* boundary in a state of *Ihram* must wear two unhemmed, unstitched white cotton/terrycloth sheets (*Rida* and *Izar*) with sandals exposing the ankle bone and instep; stitched clothing, underwear, socks, and head coverings are religiously prohibited in *Ihram*. Women in *Ihram* wear full-length loose modest attire (commonly white or black *abaya* and *hijab*) covering everything except the face and hands (niqab and gloves are prohibited during *Ihram*).
- **Penalties & Risks**: Administrative fines under the Public Decency Schedule: **SAR 100** first offense (**SAR 200** repeat) for immodest dress (Violation #19); **SAR 500** first offense (**SAR 1,000** repeat) for offensive/political/profane clothing graphics; denial of entry to malls, metro, and government offices; deportation and multi-year re-entry ban for violating Haramain boundaries.

### A.4 Brunei Darussalam
- **Primary Legal Authorities**:
  - **Syariah Penal Code Order, 2013 (SPCO)**, fully enacted April 3, 2019, enforced by Religious Enforcement Officers (*Pegawai Penguatkuasa Ugama*) and the Royal Brunei Police Force. Applies to both Muslims and non-Muslims for public-order offenses.
- **Specific Statutory Offenses & Rules**:
  - **Section 197 — Indecent Behaviour / Dress in Public**: Any person who commits an act of indecent behavior (including wearing revealing, provocative, or immodest clothing that "tends to tarnish the image of Islam") in any public place is guilty of an offense.
    - *Penalty*: Fine up to **BND $2,000**, imprisonment up to **6 months**, or both (up to BND $8,000 and 2 years imprisonment for organizers under s. 197(2)).
  - **Section 198 — Man Posing as Woman or Vice Versa (Cross-Dressing Ban)**:
    - *Section 198(1)*: Any man who dresses and poses as a woman, or any woman who dresses and poses as a man, in any public place without reasonable excuse commits a criminal offense. *Penalty*: Fine up to **BND $1,000**, imprisonment up to **3 months**, or both.
    - *Section 198(2)*: If committed "for an immoral purpose," penalty increases to a fine up to **BND $4,000**, imprisonment up to **1 year**, or both.
  - **Government Buildings & Mosques (Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque & Jame' Asr Hassanil Bolkiah Mosque)**:
    - Mandatory *tudung* (headscarf) for Muslim women in government and educational institutions. Foreign visitors entering mosques must wear provided full-length black robes (*jubah*) over clothing, and women must cover their hair. All businesses and tourist sites close completely between **12:00 PM and 2:00 PM on Fridays** under Section 194.

### A.5 Indonesia: Aceh Province (Sharia Autonomy) vs. Bali (Hindu Customary Law)
- **Aceh Province (Special Autonomous Region)**:
  - **Primary Legal Authorities**: **Qanun Aceh No. 11/2002** (Implementation of Islamic Creed, Worship, and Symbols, Art. 13) and **Qanun Aceh No. 6/2014 (*Qanun Jinayat* / Islamic Criminal Code)**, enforced by the Sharia Police (**Wilayatul Hisbah — WH**) and Satpol PP.
  - **Mandatory Attire**:
    - **Muslims**: Mandatory Islamic dress (*busana Islami*) in all public spaces. Women must wear a *jilbab*/*hijab* covering hair and chest, paired with a loose long-sleeved top and loose ankle-length skirt or trousers (**tight jeans, leggings, and short sleeves are prohibited**; WH conducts roadside *razia* checkpoints to cut or confiscate tight trousers and issue sarongs). Men are prohibited from wearing shorts above the knee in public.
    - **Non-Muslims & Foreign Tourists**: While non-Muslims can elect prosecution under the national Criminal Code (KUHP) or Qanun Jinayat, regional decrees in Banda Aceh and Sabang (Pulau Weh) require all visitors to dress modestly in public streets (covered shoulders, chest, and knees; no bikinis on public beaches outside designated private resort compounds on Pulau Weh).
    - **Motorbike Straddling Ban (Lhokseumawe Municipal Circular 002/2013)**: Adult women in Lhokseumawe are prohibited from straddling behind a male motorbike driver unless wearing modest loose attire or sitting side-saddle.
  - **Penalties**: Public reprimand, mandatory re-education, temporary detention at WH headquarters, confiscation of garments, or public caning (*uqubat cambuk*) for repeat Sharia offenses accompanied by *ikhtilat* (illicit proximity) or *khalwat*.
- **Bali Province (Hindu *Adat* & Gubernatorial Circular No. 4/2023 on Foreign Tourists)**:
  - **Primary Legal Authorities**: Bali Governor Circular Letter (*Surat Edaran*) No. 4 of 2023 ("New Order for Foreign Tourists in Bali") and *Awig-Awig Desa Adat* (customary village law).
  - **Rules**: Tourists are strictly prohibited from entering sacred temple zones (*Utama Mandala* and *Madya Mandala* of any *Pura*) without wearing traditional Balinese temple attire (**Kamben** sarong and **Selendang** waist sash), prohibited from climbing sacred trees/structures or posing nude/semi-nude at sacred sites (Mount Agung, Mount Batur, Pura Besakih, Pura Lempuyang), and required to cover up with a shirt/sarong when riding scooters or walking on public village streets away from immediate beach sand.
  - **Penalties**: Immediate deportation by Indonesian Immigration (*Imigrasi*) and criminal prosecution under **Article 156a of the Indonesian Criminal Code (KUHP)** (Blasphemy/Desecration, up to 5 years imprisonment).

### A.6 Conflict, De-Facto & Sub-National Sharia Jurisdictions
- **Yemen (Houthi-Controlled Northern Yemen / Sana'a vs. Southern Yemen / Aden)**:
  - **Houthi (*Ansar Allah*) Authorities in Sana'a, Ibb & Hodeidah**: Enforce strict *Mudawwana* (Code of Conduct) directives since 2021–2024. Women are required to wear a loose, plain **black abaya** and *hijab*/*lithma* (face veil). Houthi morality patrols (*Zaynabiyat* female police) raid clothing shops to ban colored, embroidered, or belted abayas, mannequin displays, and require female humanitarian workers to travel with a male *mahram*. Men are prohibited from wearing shorts or Western haircut fades.
- **Gaza Strip**:
  - Judicial Council and Ministry of Awqaf guidelines enforce conservative Islamic dress for women in public schools, universities, and courts (*jilbab* and *hijab*), prohibit men from walking shirtless in public streets or wearing low-slung/ripped trousers, and mandate gender segregation on beaches.
- **Somalia**:
  - **Federal Government Areas (Mogadishu) & Somaliland (Hargeisa)**: Strict customary and ministerial modesty; women wear full-length *dirac*/*guntiino* with *garbasaar* shawl or full *jilbab*/*hijab* (trousers for women in public streets trigger police or community intervention).
  - **Al-Shabaab Controlled Rural Territories (South-Central Somalia)**: Mandatory heavy thick cloth *jilbab* (often dark blue, brown, or black) with *niqab* for women; total ban on bras deemed "deceptive"; men must wear trousers cut **above the ankles** (*isbal* rule) with untrimmed beards and are executed or lashed for wearing Western sports jerseys or shorts.
- **Sudan & Mauritania**:
  - **Sudan**: Although the 1991 Criminal Act Article 152 ("indecent and immoral dress" carrying 40 lashes) was formally repealed in July 2020 by the transitional government, wartime emergency military decrees (SAF and RSF zones, 2023–2026) enforce strict checkpoint modesty for women (*toub* or *abaya* + *hijab*) and ban men's shorts.
  - **Mauritania**: Islamic Republic constitution; women wear the full-body wraparound **Melahfa** in public, and men wear the **Draa** (*boubou*) or long trousers; shorts and sleeveless tops for either sex are socially and administratively taboo.
- **Rural Pakistan (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa [KPK], Former FATA & Balochistan)**:
  - While urban enclaves in Islamabad, Lahore, and Karachi tolerate varied Pakistani attire (*shalwar kameez* with *dupatta*, or modest Western trousers), in KPK, Swat, Waziristan, and Balochistan, strict *Pashtunwali* (*Purdah*) and religious norms require women to wear a shuttlecock *burqa*, *chador*, or full *abaya*+*niqab* outside the home, and men exclusively wear *shalwar kameez* with a *pakol*, *kufi*, or turban (Western shorts on adult men are never worn).
- **Malaysia (Kelantan & Terengganu PAS State Islamic Enactments & Federal Government Premises)**:
  - **Primary Legal Authorities**: **Kelantan Syariah Criminal Code (I) Enactment 2019 (Section 29 — Indecent Act/Attire in Public Place)** and **Kota Bharu Municipal Council (MPKB-BRI) Trade Licensing By-Laws**; **Terengganu Syariah Criminal Offences (Takzir) Enactment 2001 (Section 34 & 35 — Exposing Body / Man Posing as Woman)**.
  - **Rules**: Muslim residents and visitors in Kelantan and Terengganu must cover the *aurat* (women: full body except face and hands; men: navel to knee, including a ban on Muslim men wearing shorts above the knee in public sports/streets). Non-Muslim business owners in Kelantan have been fined by MPKB for wearing shorts above the knee inside their own shops.
  - **Federal Government Buildings, Hospitals & Police Stations Nationwide**: Strict administrative dress code enforced by security guards (*Rukun Negara* fifth principle: Courtesy and Morality). Neither men nor women may enter a Malaysian police station, court, hospital, or ministry wearing shorts, mini-skirts above the knee, sleeveless tops, or flip-flops.

### A.7 Republic of Maldives
- **Primary Legal Authorities**:
  - **Maldives Penal Code (Law No. 9/2014, Section 615 — Public Indecency)** & **Ministry of Tourism Regulation on Guesthouses and Inhabited Islands**.
- **Two-Zone Legal Regime**:
  1. **Uninhabited Private Resort Islands & Liveaboard Vessels**: Exempted tourist zones where bikinis, speedos, and resort wear are freely permitted.
  2. **Capital City Malé, Hulhumalé, Domestic Airports & All Local Inhabited Islands (e.g., Maafushi, Thoddoo, Dhigurah)**:
     - **Strict Bikini & Swimwear Ban**: Wearing a bikini, two-piece swimsuit, shirtless men's swimwear, or sheer beachwear on public streets or non-designated public beaches of any inhabited local island is a **criminal offense**.
     - **Designated "Bikini Beaches"**: Local islands with guesthouses fence off a specific palm-screened "Tourist / Bikini Beach" where Western swimwear is permitted. Tourists must cover shoulders and knees (T-shirt and shorts/sarong) immediately upon stepping off the Bikini Beach onto island paths.
- **Penalties & Risks**: Police arrest (as documented in high-profile tourist arrests on Maafushi), fines, and deportation.

## Domain F: Gang, Cartel, Paramilitary & Territorial Faction Dress Codes (Severe Physical Danger Zones)

In areas controlled by non-state armed groups, drug cartels, *maras*, or prison-derived factions, **state laws are superseded on the street by lethal territorial dress codes**. Wearing the wrong brand, number, color, or headgear can result in immediate execution, kidnapping, or arbitrary military arrest.

### F.1 Brazil (Rio de Janeiro, Salvador/Bahia, Fortaleza/Ceará & São Paulo Favelas)
- **Territorial Context**: Over 4 million Brazilians live inside territories controlled or contested by major armed factions: **Comando Vermelho (CV)**, **Terceiro Comando Puro (TCP)**, **Amigos dos Amigos (ADA)**, **Primeiro Comando da Capital (PCC)**, **Bonde do Maluco (BDM)** in Bahia, and **Guardiões do Estado (GDE)** in Ceará.
- **Lethal Faction Brand, Color & Symbol Restrictions**:
  1. **Adidas "Three Stripes" (*Três Listras*)**: Associated with the number **3** (*Tudo 3*), which represents **Terceiro Comando Puro (TCP)** in Rio de Janeiro and **Bonde do Maluco (BDM)** in Salvador/Bahia (as well as GDE in Ceará). Wearing Adidas sneakers, three-stripe track pants, or three-stripe jerseys inside a **Comando Vermelho (CV)** territory (which enforces *Tudo 2*) can trigger violent interrogation, beating, or execution.
  2. **Nike Swoosh & "Two" Symbols**: Conversely, Nike gear or red attire associated with *Tudo 2* (CV) is targeted inside TCP/BDM strongholds.
  3. **Color Red (*Vermelho*)**: The signature color of **Comando Vermelho (CV)**. Wearing solid red shirts, red caps, or red backpacks into TCP, ADA, or *Milícia* (paramilitary police militia) territories in Rio de Janeiro's West or North Zones is high-risk.
  4. **Mickey Mouse / Disney Graphics**: Associated with Rio's TCP stronghold in *Complexo da Maré* (*Baile da Disney*) and the *A Tropa* faction in Bahia. In Salvador and Fortaleza, young people wearing Mickey Mouse T-shirts have been murdered by rival CV members who view the character as a rival faction insignia.
  5. **Star of David / Israeli Symbols**: Co-opted by the neo-Pentecostal drug faction **TCP ("Complexo de Israel")** in Rio's North Zone (Parada de Lucas, Vigário Geral).
  6. **Clown (*Palhaço*), Joker (*Coringa*), and Carp (*Carpa*) Tattoos**: Across Brazil, a tattoo of a **clown or the Joker** is universally recognized by both police and gangs as the symbol of a *matador de polícia* (cop-killer) affiliated with the PCC/CV. Foreigners or locals with Joker/clown tattoos or shirts face extreme aggression during Military Police (*Polícia Militar* / BOPE) frisking.
- **Mandatory Favela Entry Vehicle & Helmet Protocol**:
  - When entering any favela or peripheral community by motorcycle or car:
    - **Motorcyclists MUST immediately remove their helmet (*tirar o capacete*)** so armed lookouts (*olheiros*) can see their face. Entering a favela wearing a closed helmet is interpreted as a rival hitman (*motoqueiro*) or undercover police raid and regularly results in fatal gunfire.

    - **Kerala Temples (Padmanabhaswamy, Guruvayur)**: Require *Mundu/Dhoti* bare-chested for men and *Saree/Mundu* for women; hard-block trousers and jeans.

11. **`GUARDRAIL_MYANMAR_BAREFOOT_AND_SIKH_GURDWARA`**:
    - **Myanmar Pagodas (Shwedagon, Bagan)**: Issue **strictly barefoot (zero shoes, zero socks)** rule + shoulder-to-knee coverage.
    - **Sikh Gurdwaras (Golden Temple, Global)**: Require **head covering (*Rumāl*/*Dastar*/*Chunni*) for all genders**, barefoot entry (no socks), and zero tobacco/alcohol in pockets/bags.
12. **`GUARDRAIL_JAIN_ZERO_LEATHER`**: For all **Jain Temples** (Ranakpur, Dilwara, Palitana, Shravanabelagola), **hard-block all leather items** (leather belts, leather shoes, leather watch straps, leather bags/wallets) and require shoulder-to-ankle modest attire.
