# Marketplace & Circular Reselling

Sell, swap, or donate garments to promote circular fashion.

## Overview
List unused closet items for sale, exchange, or donation, complete with automated pricing details and secure checkout integrations.

## Prerequisites
- Verifiable email address.
- A PayPal account (for receiving seller payouts).

## Step-by-Step
1. **List Item**: Select a closet item and click "List on Market".
2. **Define Pricing**: Set the price and choose the transaction mode (Sale, Swap, or Donation).
3. **Review Fees**: Review platform fee structures (7% platform commission after standard processing).
4. **Accept Offers**: Review buyer messages, swap proposals, or coordinate donation handoffs.

## Expected Results
Instantly posted public listings with secure PayPal transaction checkout integrations.

## Troubleshooting
- **Cannot list item**: Verify you haven't reached your listing slots (Free users get standard limits; Pro gets unlimited slots).
- **Payment stuck**: Ensure your PayPal email is entered correctly in seller settings.

## Limitations
- Physical shipping and carrier tracking must be arranged independently by the seller and buyer.
