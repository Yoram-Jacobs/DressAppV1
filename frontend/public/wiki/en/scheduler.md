# Morning Schedule & Push Alerts

Start your day with automatic, weather-appropriate style recommendations delivered directly to your device.

## Overview
The Morning Scheduler automates your outfit selection by delivering custom style suggestions every morning. It checks local weather forecasts and your daily activities (via Google Calendar) to generate three matching options. Tap the notification to view the options on your personal avatar, save your favorite choice, and instantly view weather-matching scores.

## Prerequisites
- **Notifications Allowed**: Push notifications must be enabled for DressApp in your device or browser settings.
- **Closet Items**: You must have at least one top, one bottom, and one footwear item uploaded to your closet.
- **Google Calendar**: A linked Google Calendar account (optional, but recommended to make suggestions event-aware).
- **Gemini Key**: A custom Gemini API key configured in your settings.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Enable Alerts**: Go to **Profile settings** -> **Scheduler & Push**. Toggle the notification switch to enabled.
2. **Set Timing**: Set the exact hour and minute you want to receive your suggestion (e.g., 07:30 AM).
3. **Link Calendar**: Under Calendar Settings, connect your Google Calendar account so the AI knows your schedule.
4. **Open Suggestion**: When the morning push alert arrives, click it. You will be routed directly to the **Daily Suggestion** (Match) tab under **Stylist**.
5. **View Options**: The **Schedule Outfit** selector will open automatically, displaying your three styled combinations rendered directly on your avatar.
6. **Save and Review**: Tap any of the daily suggestions to schedule it on your calendar. The app will save the outfit and immediately slide open a detail panel showing its weather-compatibility metrics (color harmony, temperature fit, and style consistency).

## Expected Results
A notification is delivered daily at your chosen time. Clicking it opens the app, displays three options on your avatar, and lets you save one to your calendar with full compatibility details.

## Troubleshooting
- **No notifications arriving**: 
  - Ensure notifications are allowed for the DressApp website in your browser's site settings or your operating system's settings.
  - Verify that your device is not in "Do Not Disturb" or "Focus" mode during your scheduled notification time.
- **Missing clothing items on the avatar**: 
  - Make sure you have clothes in all basic categories (tops, bottoms, shoes) in your closet so the scheduler can dress the avatar properly.
- **Generic recommendations**:
  - Link your Google Calendar so the suggestions match your specific daily events.

## Limitations
- You can schedule up to one outfit per day on your calendar.
- Weather updates require an active internet connection on the server.
