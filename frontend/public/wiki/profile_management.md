# Profile, Sizing & Configuration

Fine-tune your measurements, modesty constraints, and AI credentials.

## Overview
The Profile section keeps your styling context up-to-date, managing body parameters, styling rules, custom keys, and local region settings.

## Prerequisites
- Active DressApp user account.

## Step-by-Step
1. **Enter Metrics**: Update your height, shoulder, chest, and hip values.
2. **Specify Rules**: Select style avoids (e.g., "avoid yellow") and modesty levels.
3. **AI Configuration**: Input your custom Google AI Studio keys.
4. **Manage Account**: View your subscription level (Pro vs Free limit of 150 items) or request account deletion.

## Expected Results
Personalized outfit layouts conforming to your exact shape and clothing style preferences.

## Troubleshooting
- **API key invalid**: Verify that you copied the key correctly from Google AI Studio without extra spaces.
- **Calendar not sync**: Unlink and re-authenticate your Google account to refresh tokens.

## Limitations
- Custom rules are applied strictly; if your rules are too strict, the stylist may find no matching outfits.
